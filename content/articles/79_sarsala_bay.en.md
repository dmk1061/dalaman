# Sarsala Bay: A Turquoise Pine Horseshoe 25 Minutes From Airport Departures

Every summer morning, planeloads of holidaymakers pour straight out of [Dalaman Airport (`DLM`)](/en/articles/airport-dalaman) down busy highways toward Marmaris and Fethiye—completely oblivious that tucked right behind the pine ridge on the Kapıdağ Peninsula, barely **15–20 kilometers (25–30 minutes)** from the runway, gleams one of the Gulf of Fethiye's loveliest sheltered turquoise bays: **Sarsala Bay (*Sarsala Koyu*)**.

Once a secret refuge known exclusively to yacht captains, paved mountain tarmac now opens this pine-cradled horseshoe cleanly to rental sedans—making it unbeatable either as a quiet beach escape or the single smartest final afternoon swim before dropping luggage back at `DLM` check-in desks.

## Big Sarsala vs. Little Sarsala: Don't Confuse Both Coves

Pinched smoothly inside forested crags keeping rough ocean swells out year-round, water here glows vivid emerald-turquoise—just distinguish two neighboring stops on charts and boat chatter:

1. **Big Sarsala (*Büyük Sarsala*) — Drive-In Municipal Beach:** The sweeping main crescent fitted with a timber jetty, public amenities, and crisp open swim bay. Expect **smooth medium-to-fine pebbles** (zero sand, so pack rubber aqua shoes to save soles!) dropping quite briskly into glass-clear depth sparkling brightly five or six meters out. A low-key lakeside-style municipal café grills simple snacks beside fresh showers, changing stalls, toilets, and sunbeds minus thumping beach-club DJs or noisy jet skis.
2. **Little Sarsala (*Küçük Sarsala*) — Yacht-Only Cove Next Door:** Round the immediate rocky headland lies an intimate hidden cove anchored by timber catwalks and a beloved skippers' seafood tavern overhanging turquoise shallows—inaccessible comfortably by regular cars and visited almost solely aboard charter boats.

## Reaching Sarsala: Steep Pine Hairpins vs. Sailing In

* **Driving From [Dalaman](/en/dalaman) or [Sarıgerme](/en/articles/sarigerme-guide):** Follow brown **`Sarsala`** signs through Kapıkargın orchards. Initial miles roll effortlessly through fragrant pine and Ice-Age sweetgum valleys—yet the **final 5–6 kilometers plunge down a steep, narrow, unlit cliffside mountain switchback** stacked with tight blind hairpins. Keep speed down gently in lower gears, hold wide eye-contact approaching oncoming vans near passing pockets, and expect a modest seasonal municipal barrier fee at beach entry.
* **Arriving by Yacht From Göcek or Fethiye:** Sarsala remains a staple afternoon swim stop aboard classic "12 Islands" gulets and skippered day charters out of [Göcek](/en/gocek) and [Fethiye](/en/fethiye) ([coastal yachting guide](/en/articles/yachting) & [islands overview](/en/articles/islands-bays)). Stick around past **16:00** as day cruisers weigh anchor, and hushed stillness reclaims the pine valley cleanly.

## Five Practical Tips Before Packing the Boot

* **Bring Goggles & Small Lira Notes:** Rocky shelves hugging both headland walls swarm cleanly with wrasse, sea bream, and darting octopuses—snorkeling beats shallow sandy beaches miles ahead! Carry paper lira bills because weak cellular bars inside mountain walls frequently freeze wireless card terminals at entry gates and café counters.
* **Great Day Out—Not a Week-Long Resort Base:** Zero hotels, street shops, or pharmacies border Big Sarsala—treat it as an effortless half- or full-day picnic paired easily with secluded **[Kille Bay](/en/articles/kille-bay)** next ridge over (families seeking week-long sandy amenities sleep happier at nearby **[Sarıgerme](/en/articles/sarigerme-guide)**).

> [!TIP]
> **The Coast's Smartest Pre-Flight Hack:** Returning an afternoon or evening **[rental car](/en/articles/vehicle-rental-cars-scooters-bikes)** to `DLM` after an annoying 11:00 hotel checkout in Fethiye or Kaş? Never spend four hot hours slumped inside airport café lines! Drive straight into **Sarsala** with suitcases tucked cleanly in your locked boot: swim three dreamy hours in crystal water, rinse clean under fresh showers, grab cool pine-shaded lunch, and cruise just **25 smooth minutes directly onto your Dalaman departure curb**!

## Timing & Twilight Road Safety

Wrapped tight by steep mountains, Sarsala swings cleanly across coastal seasons ([check weather guide](/en/articles/climate-and-seasons)):

* **Best Visit Windows:** **May–June** and **September–October** deliver peak water warmth and sparse boats. Mid-summer packs the wooden pier tightly with day-cruise boats from **11:30 to 15:30**—arrive early or stay cleanly after **16:00** for glowing golden solitude right to sunset.
* **Dusk Switchback Alert:** Pack closed sneakers if scrambling headland trails above the bay for aerial photos ([photo guide](/en/articles/photo-guide-best-instagram-spots)), aqua shoes, sunblock, and plenty of water. Driving back up the unlit mountain switchback past dusk? Watch sharp roadside drops carefully—wild boar and slow land tortoises occasionally wander warm asphalt after dark!
