# Yachting & Boat Trips: Navigating the Coast Between Datça and Kaş

Along this serrated corner of southwest Turkey, the sea doubles as a coastal highway—faster, breezier, and wildly prettier than twisting mountain roads inland. Between [Marmaris](/en/marmaris), [Göcek](/en/gocek), and [Fethiye](/en/fethiye) floats the Eastern Mediterranean's largest leisure and charter fleet, spanning mega-yacht marinas, classic twin-masted handcrafted wooden **gulets**, and laid-back family-run day boats.

Even nervous sailors love these waters. Chains of pine-clad islands shield the **Gulf of Fethiye–Göcek** and the **Gulf of Gökova** from open-sea swell, leaving coves as glassy as mountain lakes only 15–45 minutes apart under sail or motor.

## Göcek: The Yachting Capital 25 Minutes From Arrivals

Just 22 km west of [Dalaman](/en/dalaman) Airport lies **[Göcek](/en/gocek)** ([full town guide](/en/articles/gocek-guide)), a polished harbor village built entirely around life afloat. No fewer than **six world-class marinas** (led by *D-Marin Göcek*, *Marintürk*, and *Skopea*) line its sheltered shore—yet municipal zoning caps all town buildings at two stories, keeping skyscrapers out of sight.

Göcek is ground zero for exploring the famed **12 Islands**: a single relaxed loop sails past Tersane Island's ruined Ottoman shipyard, shallow Yassıca sandbars, Bedri Rahmi Bay (named after the Turkish painter's fish mural on a lakeside-style rock), and sunken Byzantine stones in Hamam Bay ("Cleopatra's Bath"). Across the entire gulf, Turkey's strict **Mavi Kart** blackwater tracking system requires every charter boat to pump sewage tanks at port stations—keeping even peak August anchorages crystal clear.

## Marmaris & Datça: Regattas, Bays & Crossing to Greek Symi

* **[Marmaris](/en/marmaris):** One of Turkey's oldest sailing powerhouses (*Netsel Marina*, *Albatros*, *Marmaris Yacht Marina*), launching week-long bareboat charters into the Gulf of Gökova and around the wild Bozburun Peninsula. Every late October–November, over a hundred international racing crews pack the harbor for **Marmaris International Race Week**. Without a skipper's ticket, grab any affordable daily harbor boat out to Turunç, Kumlubük, Çiftlik, and Cennet (Paradise) Island.
* **[Datça Peninsula](/en/articles/datca-peninsula):** Quieter waters for purists. Local boats run day trips from Datça harbor out to the twin ports of ancient **Knidos** and empty coves across the Gulf of Hisarönü. From late spring into early autumn, a seasonal passenger ferry zips across to pastel-painted Greek **Symi** in just **45–55 minutes** (sailings don't run daily—carry a valid passport and verify current Schengen entry/ETIAS/visa rules for your nationality before buying tickets).

## Fethiye, Ölüdeniz & Kaş: Day Trips vs. Classic Blue Cruises

You don't need a private yacht budget to spend all day on the water:

* **12 Islands Day Cruise (Fethiye / Göcek):** The staple **10:00–17:30** itinerary with 4–5 swim stops and freshly grilled chicken/fish lunch on deck runs **€25–35 per person** on shared boats in 2026 (bar drinks billed separately).
* **Ölüdeniz Lagoon & Caves:** Boats pull directly off Belcekız sand for **[Butterfly Valley](/en/articles/butterfly-valley)**, Byzantine **[St. Nicholas Island (Gemiler)](/en/articles/st-nicholas-island)**, and Aquarium Cove.
* **Kaş & Üçağız to Kekova:** The eastern coast's signature cruise glides smoothly over the sunken Lycian houses of **Dolichiste**, stops beneath car-free **Simena Castle (Kaleköy)** for homemade goat-milk ice cream, and anchors in turquoise **Tersane Bay**.
* **The Blue Cruise (*Mavi Yolculuk*):** The timeless Turkish ritual—3 to 7 nights living aboard a traditional wide-beamed wooden gulet. You wake up anchored in a fresh pine-ringed cove each dawn while the crew lays out slow-cooked *meze* and sea bass on deck. The punchiest classic taster is the **4-day / 3-night** cabin charter from **Fethiye → Butterfly Valley → Kaş → Kekova → Demre** (and back), starting around **€350–500 per berth** depending on season and cabin standard.

## Picking the Right Boat Without Regretting It at Sea

> [!TIP]
> **Insider Tip:** Two completely different worlds bob side by side along Fethiye, Marmaris, and Ölüdeniz quays for nearly the exact same ticket price. Towering multi-deck "pirate" galleons sell **high-volume DJ sets, foam cannons, and party games** for 100+ passengers. Prefer cicadas, pine scent, and actual conversation in quiet coves? Look explicitly for **Quiet / No Music Boat** signs or classic small timber boats capped at **10–18 guests**. Traveling as **6–8 friends or family**? Ask captains on the quay about a **private day boat charter** (**~€250–450 total** including crew, fuel, and freshly cooked lunch)—splitting the tab buys complete freedom to skip crowded stops and linger where no tour boat anchors.
