# Dalaman Airport (DLM): Terminals, Shuttles & Getting to Your Resort

Unlike sprawling Antalya Airport, where walking from the jet bridge to the street can eat up an hour and a half, compact **Dalaman Airport (`DLM`)**—just 6 km south of [Dalaman](/en/dalaman) town—is refreshingly traveler-friendly. Step off your plane, and you can be sipping espresso beside a Göcek marina or strolling onto Sarıgerme sand in under an hour.

`DLM`'s biggest edge is simple geography: it sits dead center along the Datça-to-Kaş coastline. Its sleek International Terminal, opened in 2018 under operator YDA, handles seasonal charters and direct flights from across the UK, mainland Europe, and beyond (April through October), while the adjacent Domestic Terminal keeps the coast linked year-round via Istanbul (both IST and SAW) and Ankara.

## Two Terminals Side by Side: Navigation, Wi-Fi & Money

Dalaman runs two passenger buildings sitting almost shoulder to shoulder: the modern **International Terminal (T2)** and the older **Domestic Terminal (T1)**. Walking outdoors along the curbside between them takes only 3–5 minutes—handy both if you're connecting via Istanbul or arriving internationally and heading for the airport shuttle stop outside T1.

* **Free Wi-Fi:** Log on to `DLM free Wi-Fi` in either terminal for up to **240 minutes** of free access after a quick browser sign-in (no roaming SMS? Use the interactive info kiosks in the arrival hall to print an access code).
* **Currency & SIM Cards:** Baggage claim and arrivals house exchange booths plus Turkcell, Vodafone, and Türk Telekom stands. Airport exchange spreads and tourist SIM packages carry hefty markups compared to town centers: swap just €20–30 / £20–30 (or pull a small stash of lira from an ATM) for immediate coffee and bus fare, then change the bulk of your cash in town.
* **Food & Storage:** Airside cafes past passport control price menus in euros at steep airport rates. If you have time before an afternoon flight, eat in Dalaman town at a fraction of the price. Left-luggage lockers and wrapping services operate 24/7 in departures.

## Havaş & Muttaş Buses: Budget Links to Fethiye & Marmaris

Regular city *dolmuş* minibuses do **not** pull directly into the airport. Budget travelers rely on full-size coach shuttles run by **MUTTAŞ** (Muğla's municipal carrier) and **Havaş**. Both park directly outside **Domestic Terminal T1**—exit International Arrivals (T2), turn right along the sidewalk, and walk three minutes to the coach bays.

* **How timetables work:** Instead of rigid clock-face departures, airport coaches sync directly to incoming **domestic flights** (Turkish Airlines, Pegasus, AJet), rolling out roughly 20–25 minutes after touchdown once bags clear. Note that if you land on a late-night international charter with no matching domestic flight nearby, you may face a wait—or prefer a pre-booked transfer or taxi.
* **Two primary routes:**
  * **Westbound → Marmaris Otogar:** runs via the Ortaca junction, Köyceğiz, and Gökova to Marmaris main bus terminal (~95 km, 75–90 min). 2026 fare: **500 ₺ (~€9)** all the way to Marmaris; **260 ₺ (~€4.70)** to Ortaca; **320 ₺ (~€5.80)** to Köyceğiz.
  * **Eastbound → Fethiye Otogar:** runs through the Göcek Tunnel and Göcek town to Fethiye bus station (~49 km, 45–60 min). 2026 fare: **360 ₺ (~€6.50–7)** to Fethiye; **260 ₺ (~€4.70)** to Göcek.
* **Tickets & onward connections:** No advance online booking is required—pay the driver or kiosk attendant by cash (TRY) or contactless bank card at boarding. Coaches drop off at central bus terminals (*Otogar*), where local *dolmuş* vans connect straight onward to Ölüdeniz, İçmeler, Dalyan, or Kaş.

## Taxis & Private Transfers: Real Resort Fares (2026)

Step outside arrivals and you'll find yellow cabs of the official `Dalaman Havalimanı Taksiciler Kooperatifi`. Within Muğla Province, metered trips follow **UKOME** rates (**50 ₺** flag drop + **35 ₺/km**, minimum ~**480 ₺** around the airport district), while longer resort runs follow posted cooperative zone rates displayed on a large signboard outside arrivals.

Traveling as three or four people? Pre-booking a [private airport transfer](/en/services/transfers) to Dalyan, Fethiye, or Kaş often matches or beats curb taxi rates—with flight tracking, meet-and-greet at arrivals, child seats, and plenty of trunk space thrown in.

## Terminal Car Rentals & the Göcek Tunnel Catch

Inside the arrivals hall, global brands (Avis, Budget, Europcar, Hertz, Sixt, Enterprise) and major Turkish fleets (Garenta, Çizgi) keep 24-hour desks, while local agencies hand over keys right at the terminal curb. Pulling out onto the coastal highway puts you instantly on the **D400**: turn left toward Dalyan, Marmaris, and Datça; turn right for Göcek, Fethiye, and Kaş.

> [!TIP]
> **Insider Tip:** Driving east from the airport toward Göcek, Fethiye, or Kaş takes you straight through the **Göcek Tunnel (*Göcek Tüneli*)**—one of the coast's few private toll tunnels (**75 ₺ / ~€1.35** for a standard car in 2026). **Booths do not accept cash:** either your rental car carries an electronic **HGS** windshield tag (settled when you return the car) or an attendant taps your contactless bank card at the gate. In daylight with zero rush, you can swing onto the free old mountain pass above the tunnel for sweeping aerial views over Göcek Bay.
