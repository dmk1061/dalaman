# Kekova & Simena (Kaleköy): Sunken Lycian Streets & a Car-Free Castle Village

Roughly 33 kilometers east of bohemian [Kaş](/en/kas), the Mediterranean coastline breaks into a glassy maze of limestone inlets and pine islands hiding one of the most memorable sights in Turkey. Rather than resort promenades, this stretch protects the **sunken Lycian city of Dolichiste** off **Kekova Island** and the sleepy hillside castle village of **Kaleköy (ancient Simena)**—reachable with **zero road traffic**.

Because the harbor sits roughly **180 km** (~2 hr 30 min to 2 hr 45 min) from [Dalaman Airport (`DLM`)](/en/articles/airport-dalaman), most travelers visit Kekova from a base in Kaş or [Kalkan](/en/articles/kalkan-guide), turning it into the standout sea day of their entire eastern trip.

## Sunken City of Kekova: Protected Zone & Swimming Rules

In the 2nd century AD, violent earthquakes fractured the shoreline, sliding the northern waterfront of Dolichiste settlement beneath crystal-clear turquoise waves. Today, peering down from the water surface clearly reveals stone staircases running straight into the sea, house foundations, sunken amphora shards, and harbor walls.

* **Strict No-Swim Protection:** Kekova Island's sunken ruins form a strictly guarded marine archaeological reserve. **Swimming, snorkeling, scuba diving, and anchoring directly over the submerged ruins are prohibited.** Boats throttle down to idle speed and glide slowly parallel to shore so passengers view the ruins cleanly from deck.
* **Where Boats Stop to Swim:** Every day cruise balances the ruins pass with long, leisurely swim breaks in gorgeous open-access bays nearby—especially glassy **Aquarium Bay (*Akvaryum Koyu*)**, the submerged Byzantine church ruins at **Tersane Bay**, and sheltered Gökkaya inlet.
* **Dawn Sea Kayaking:** The quietest, closest view without engine noise belongs to guided morning sea kayaks launching early from Üçağız village ([coastal diving & sea sports guide](/en/articles/diving)).

## Kaleköy (Simena): The 300-Seat Theater & Sarcophagus in the Sea

Directly facing Kekova Island across the narrow channel clings tiny **Kaleköy** ("Castle Village"). No asphalt road reaches town—visitors arrive exclusively **by boat from Üçağız or Kaş**, or walk roughly 30–40 minutes down a rocky footpath from an inland parking lot above the hill.

1. **The Floating Lycian Sarcophagus:** An iconic symbol of southern Turkey stands right in the shallows near village wooden jetties—an ornate limestone Lycian house-tomb resting on a stone plinth surrounded by gentle ripples and circling sea kayaks.
2. **Knights' Castle & Micro-Theater:** Climb steep stone staircases past flower-draped lanes selling woven linen scarves to reach the medieval fortress atop ancient Simena's acropolis. Tucked right inside the castle walls is the **smallest theater in Lycia**, carved directly into living rock with seven rows seating roughly **300 spectators**. From the upper battlements, panoramic views sweep across the island channel and a hillside necropolis studded with ancient sarcophagi.
3. **Homemade Goat-Milk Ice Cream:** Family-run cafés lining the path below the fortress churn thick, richly flavored homemade goat-milk ice cream in local seasonal flavors like fig, peach, almond, and lemon.
4. **Waterfront Dockside Dining:** Village tavernas place wooden tables right on harbor piers where passing sailboats moor directly alongside lunch guests.

## Three Ways to Visit Kekova & Simena (2026)

Choosing how you approach the bay dramatically shifts the experience:

* **Full-Day Boat Cruise from Kaş or Üçağız:** Classic ~10:00–17:30 day gulets include freshly cooked lunch aboard plus four swimming coves. Departing from Kaş involves ~1 hr 15 min open-sea sailing each way; launching from tiny **Üçağız harbor** cuts boat transit to 10–15 minutes—ideal for self-drivers wanting minimal transit and maximum cove time.
* **Private Small Boat from Üçağız:** Drive ~35 minutes from Kaş straight to Üçağız pier and hire a local wooden boat captain privately with 4–8 friends or family. You control the exact schedule and climb Simena Fortress outside peak midday excursion hours ([regional yachting guide](/en/articles/yachting)).
* **Guided Morning Sea Kayak (07:30–11:30):** Launches smoothly from Üçağız with a certified guide, paddling quietly along the shallow edges of Kekova's sunken staircases before midday breezes ruffle the water.

## Practical Entry, Overnight & Budget Notes (2026)

* **Simena Fortress Tickets (Inflation Shield):** Access to the castle battlements is managed by Turkey's Ministry of Culture and Tourism (`muze.gov.tr`). Foreign visitor ticket rates follow official euro benchmarks converted into Turkish Lira at the gate; entry is included on the 7-day regional **MuseumPass Akdeniz** card.
* **Consider Spending One Quiet Night:** Between 12:30 and 15:30 Kaleköy's lanes fill with day-boat crews, yet after **17:00** every excursion vessel returns to Kaş and Üçağız. A handful of tiny stone pensions stay open, leaving overnight guests dining under brilliant stars over silent water.

> [!TIP]
> **Best Self-Drive Plan:** Skip long bus transfers down to Kaş harbor if you already have a rental car. Drive directly to **Üçağız pier by 09:00**, park near the waterfront, and board either a morning kayak group or a private small wooden boat for 3–4 hours. You experience Kekova's sunken stone stairs in glass-calm morning light and climb Simena Castle ahead of peak midday tour crowds—leaving time to continue 25 minutes east toward [ancient Myra and Demre](/en/articles/myra-ancient-city).
