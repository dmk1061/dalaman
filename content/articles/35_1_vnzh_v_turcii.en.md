# Residency Permits (*İkamet*) & Moving to the Dalaman Coast in 2026

Sunny skies over [Dalaman](/en/dalaman), walkable waterfront cafés in [Fethiye](/en/fethiye), and serene riverside trails in Dalyan lure far more than two-week sunseekers—thousands of remote workers, early retirees, and families have settled across Muğla Province. Yet Turkey's once-breezy era of *"fly in, lease an apartment for six months, and collect an automatic tourist residence card"* is firmly over.

Skip outdated expat forum rumors from 2021. Here is how legal stays, **İkamet** applications, and everyday relocation logistics around Fethiye, Marmaris, and Kaş genuinely work in **2026**.

## 90/180-Day Visa-Free Stays & Main 2026 *İkamet* Routes

Most **UK, US, Canadian, and EU passport holders** can stay in Turkey visa-free for up to **90 days within any rolling 180-day window** (citizens of Australia, Ireland, and several other countries should check current e-Visa requirements at [evisa.gov.tr](https://www.evisa.gov.tr/) before departure). Planning to stay past 90 days? You need an official **İkamet** residence permit:

1. **Short-Term Touristic Residency by Rental Contract (*Kısa Dönem İkamet*):** Still exists on paper—but from 2023 into **2026**, immigration offices across coastal provinces have **routinely rejected first-time touristic rental applications** across the board. Signing a 12-month apartment lease expecting a guaranteed first residence permit carries serious financial risk unless extending an existing status or holding a clearly documented legal ground.
2. **Property-Owner Residency (*Taşınmaz Mal / TAPU*):** The clearest pathway for non-working relocators and retirees. Under strict national rules, your residential home (*konut*) must show an appraisal and *Tapu* title value of **at least $200,000 USD**, **and** sit inside an **open *mahalle* district** (where foreign residents remain below Turkey's **20% neighborhood ceiling**—always verify open vs. closed *mahalles* in Fethiye, Marmaris, and Kaş at `goc.gov.tr` before buying!).
3. **Family Residency (*Aile İkamet İzni*):** Granted to spouses and minor children of Turkish citizens or foreign residents holding eligible work/qualified permits.
4. **Work Permit (*Çalışma İzni*):** Sponsored directly by a Turkish employer (or via incorporating your own Turkish company satisfying local staff quotas). Serves as both legal residency and work authorization.
5. **Student Residency (*Öğrenci İkamet İzni*):** Issued upon enrollment at an accredited Turkish university or certified in-person Turkish language school (such as state-backed **TÖMER** centers—cheap online paper programs no longer pass inspection).

## Applying Online via *e-İkamet*: Step by Step

* **Use Only the Official State Portal:** Every legitimate application is filed strictly through **[e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr](https://e-ikamet.goc.gov.tr/)** *before* your visa-free days expire. Ignore paid Google lookalike agencies charging steep markup just to fill out the identical free web form.
* **Core Document File:**
  * Passport valid at least **60 days beyond** your requested permit end date;
  * 4 biometric passport photographs;
  * Turkish Tax Number (*Vergi Numarası*—generated free in ten minutes online via `ivd.gib.gov.tr`);
  * Valid **Turkish Private Health Insurance (*Özel Sağlık Sigortası*)** meeting statutory policy tables (home-country holiday travel policies are rejected);
  * Proof of registered address (*Tapu* deed or notarized rental contract paired with municipal *Numarataj* address verification);
  * Proof of sufficient financial means plus state tax & card fee receipts (*Harç ve Kart Bedeli*).
* **The Appointment (*Randevu*):** Submitting generates an in-person interview date at your nearest **Göç İdaresi** office (**Fethiye**, **Marmaris**, or provincial capital **Muğla**). Once approved, your pink residence card mails straight to your apartment via **PTT** registered post.

## Everyday Life After Touchdown

* **Opening a Bank Account:** Paying monthly utility, fiber internet, and mobile bills cleanly requires a Turkish bank account. Rules for non-residents without *İkamet* shift monthly, though expats report the smoothest openings at **`Ziraat Bank`**, **`VakıfBank`**, or **`DenizBank`** branches carrying a tax ID and qualifying opening deposit.
* **Smartphones & the 120-Day Clock:** Any non-Turkish phone loses signal on Turkish SIMs after **120 days per IMEI slot**. Permanent state IMEI registration sits at a steep **54,258 ₺ (~€970)** in 2026—so almost everyone relocating picks up an inexpensive locally registered Turkish phone just for +90 calls and banking SMS codes.
* **Schools & Expat Community:** Fethiye, Marmaris, and Muğla host solid state schools alongside private international-leaning colleges offering bilingual English tracks. Meanwhile, **Fethiye, Çalış, Ovacık, Dalyan, and Kaş** sustain a large, seasoned international and English-speaking expat community—making trusted recommendations for mechanics, doctors, vets, and handymen easy to find within days.

> [!TIP]
> **Insider Tip:** Before handing over an annual lease deposit or buying coastal property, **test-drive the coast in winter first**. Book a one-to-two-month trial apartment between **November and January** across Dalaman, Fethiye, or Dalyan: inspect real household dampness, test home heating, confirm current open *mahalle* streets on `goc.gov.tr`, and talk directly with local expats before committing long term.

*Disclaimer: Turkish immigration rules, income multiples, and closed-district lists change frequently without long notice. Always verify active requirements on [goc.gov.tr](https://www.goc.gov.tr/) before initiating property transfers or relocation moves.*
