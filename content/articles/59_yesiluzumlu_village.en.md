# Yeşilüzümlü & Cadianda: Pine Air, Hand Looms & Mountain Ruins Above Fethiye

Whenever midsummer pushes [Fethiye](/en/fethiye) beaches toward sticky 37 °C humidity, knowledgeable locals turn rental keys uphill—and twenty-five minutes of pine-scented hairpins drops them straight into **Yeşilüzümlü** (*"Green Grape"*). Sitting perched at **500 meters elevation**, the plateau holds air steadily **5 to 6 °C cooler** than the bay below, greeting evenings with fresh mountain drafts sliding cleanly past old Ottoman stonework and terraced vineyards.

Leave beach umbrellas and pumping pool speakers at sea level: Yeşilüzümlü trades boardwalk hustle for artisan wooden looms, boutique country wine glasses, and an entire **crowd-free ancient Lycian citadel** hidden in forest silence right above village roofs.

## Four Reasons to Spend a Full Day Up in the Hills

1. **Wooden-Loom *Dastar* Weaving Workshops:** Yeşilüzümlü stands among the last villages keeping heirloom ***dastar*** craftsmanship alive on clacking timber ***çekmen*** foot-looms. Step quietly inside village studios or municipal workshops to watch artisans feed delicate organic cotton and linen threads into wispy scarves, breezy beach shirts, and hand-fringed table runners—easily the most honest handcrafted textile along the coast ([local crafts guide](/en/articles/suveniry-i-remesla)).
2. **Boutique Village Vineyards & Cellars:** True to its name, sun-warmed hillsides circling town have nourished hardy native grapes for centuries. Tiny family wineries open stone courtyard patios for unhurried dry tastings and homemade pomegranate blends without waterfront resort markups ([Turkish wines and drinks guide](/en/articles/alkogol-v-turtsii-chto-pit-i-gde-pokupat)).
3. **April's Morel Mushroom Festival (*Kuzugöbeği Mantar Festivali*):** Every **April**, Yeşilüzümlü's sleepy cobbled square bursts alive for Turkey's beloved spring wild-foraging fair: villagers carry woven baskets bursting with earthy pine morels (*kuzugöbeği*—"lamb's belly"), threading grilled mushroom skewers over wood smoke beside *dastar* textile stalls and street musicians ([events calendar](/en/articles/events-and-festivals-calendar)).
4. **Plane-Tree Square Lunches:** Centered neatly around a shaded stone square where elderly villagers clack backgammon dice over mid-morning tea, Yeşilüzümlü's unpretentious *lokantas* simmer clay-pot meat stews (*güveç*) and slow-roasted lamb *tandır* at roughly half Fethiye harbor tariffs.

## Ancient Cadianda (*Kadyanda*): Free Forest Ruins at 900 Meters

Don't turn back downhill without driving **6–8 km higher** past town pines onto the ridge concealing **Cadianda (*Kadyanda*)**—northernmost outpost of classical Lycia along this valley.

* **Zero Coach Crowds:** Tight forested bends keep massive 50-seat excursion buses safely down on the coast, leaving sprawling ridge avenues nearly empty for unhurried morning explorers.
* **What Remains Standing:** Impressive polygonal dry-stone fortifications, a steep hillside Hellenistic **theater** wrapped in pine shadow, a **~200-meter stone stadium** flanked sharply by cliff wall on one edge and breathtaking valley drop toward Fethiye Bay along the other, brick-flue Roman baths, and a market *agora*.
* **Engineering Marvel Below the Agora:** Give yourself **90 minutes** wandering the leafy loop off the forestry parking clearings (see wider regional classics inside our [ancient cities overview](/en/articles/ancient-cities)). Be sure to peer into the **four huge vaulted underground rainwater cisterns** carved cleanly beneath the central square—high on a waterless limestone crest at 900 meters, Cadianda stayed alive solely by hoarding winter rains and melting highland snow.
* **2026 Practicalities:** Entry stays **100% free** with zero turnstiles or ticket kiosks (a local watchman sometimes secures the vehicle gate late afternoon, so visit before **16:00**). Zero cafés or taps stand inside the woods—carry full water bottles and sturdy closed sneakers across slick pine needles.

## Mountain Seasons Around the Village

Unlike coastal beach strips driven strictly by August surf, Yeşilüzümlü runs briskly all four seasons (compare monthly shifts inside our [climate guide](/en/articles/climate-and-seasons)):

* **March–May:** Luminous emerald foliage, soft valley morning mists, wild olive blossoms, and April morels—bring a light windbreaker once dusk touches +14–18 °C.
* **June–September:** Yeşilüzümlü's sharpest secret weapon. While sea-level bedrooms simmer warmly at +28 °C nights, cooling mountain air sinks pleasantly toward +20–22 °C—explaining why coastal residents rent stone vineyard cottages here for deep summer sleep.
* **October–February:** Pomegranate and olive pressing season, crisp fireside dining, young wine pours, and occasional winter frosts dusting surrounding peaks white.

> [!TIP]
> **Getting Up Without Waiting Around:** By [rental car](/en/articles/vehicle-rental-cars-scooters-bikes), follow highway signage toward *Yeşilüzümlü / Üzümlü* cleanly out of Fethiye in **25–30 minutes**. Car-free? Frequent hourly **Üzümlü** *dolmuş* vans roll up from Fethiye bus station and central mosque square—just arrange a quick village taxi with waiting time at the central square if adding the final 7 km climb up to Cadianda's forest ruins, folding everything effortlessly into a relaxed half-day escape ([day trips guide](/en/articles/day-trips-and-itineraries)).
