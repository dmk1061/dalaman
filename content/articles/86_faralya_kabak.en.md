# Faralya & Kabak Valley: Wooden Bungalows, Lycian Trails & 350m Sea Cliffs

Leave busy [Ölüdeniz Beach](/en/articles/oludeniz) behind and climb the narrow pine-clad road wrapping around the flanks of Mount Babadağ, and within fifteen minutes the coastline transforms entirely. Resort neon vanishes; steep pine ridges rise to the left while an open turquoise horizon stretches far below on the right. Welcome to the cliffside village of **Uzunyurt**—universally known as **Faralya**—and the horseshoe amphitheater of **Kabak Valley (*Kabak Koyu*)** just beyond it.

Located roughly **10–17 km** (20–35 minutes along winding cliff roads) south of Ölüdeniz and **70–75 km** (~1 hr 20 min to 1 hr 35 min) from [Dalaman Airport (`DLM`)](/en/articles/airport-dalaman), this pocket draws travelers seeking stillness, eco-lodges perched over the sea, and direct stepping-stones onto Turkey's legendary [Lycian Way trail](/en/articles/trekking).

## Faralya (Uzunyurt): Sunsets Over Butterfly Valley's 350m Drop

Spread across a string of quiet mountain hamlets (Hisar, Kızılkaya, Orta Mahalle), Faralya sits directly on the limestone lip towering above [Butterfly Valley](/en/articles/butterfly-valley):

* **Cliff-Edge Viewpoints:** Sheer rock walls drop roughly **350 meters** straight down into Butterfly Valley's sandy cove. Faralya's roadside stone outcroppings deliver the classic aerial panorama of the gorge—though it is far safer to admire the drop from established café terraces than loose crumbly cliffs in flip-flops.
* **Peaceful Cliffside Stays:** Rather than large resorts, Faralya specializes in intimate boutique villas, stone cottages with infinity plunge pools, family pensions, and quiet forest lodges. Nightlife here means unhurried terrace dinners watching the sun sink straight into the Mediterranean.
* **Gentle Village Walks:** Casual hikers can skip heavy multi-day treks and follow dirt village tracks down toward peaceful Aktaş pebble coves or wander pine pastures up toward Kirme village.

## Kabak Valley (*Kabak Koyu*): Glamping, Yoga Retreats & Getting to the Shore

A few kilometers south of Faralya, the cliff road reaches the steep rim overlooking deep, green **Kabak Bay**:

* **Timber & Canvas Instead of Concrete:** Strict conservation rules protect the valley floor from big concrete hotels, leaving pine slopes dotted with rustic wooden bungalows, stylish glamping tents, and open-air yoga decks tucked amid oleander and pine needles.
* **Two Ways Down to Kabak Beach:** Standard sedans and tour buses park along the upper roadside turning area (*Last Stop*). From there you have two options:
  1. **Walk the Lycian Trail Path:** A rocky, root-strewn footpath winds downhill through pines in **20–25 minutes**. Note that climbing back uphill under blazing midday heat takes **30–40 minutes**—bring proper sneakers and plenty of drinking water.
  2. **Local 4×4 Shuttle Jeeps:** Rugged local pickup shuttles shuttle guests and backpacks regularly between the top road, valley camps, and the beachfront along a steep dirt track for a modest per-ride fare.
* **Beach Profile & Afternoon Swell:** Kabak's sweeping crescent mixes smooth medium pebbles and coarse pale sand framed by towering pale cliffs. Mornings bring crystal-clear calm water; after **13:00–14:00**, open-sea swell frequently rolls onto shore, making morning swims best for less confident swimmers.
* **Aladere Waterfall Hike:** A signed forest trail winds deeper up the canyon behind the camps to cool rock pools and a small cascade (~45–60 minutes each way).

## Lycian Way Hiking & Neighboring Bays

The coastal corridor linking **Ovacık – Faralya – Kabak – Alınca** stands among the finest day-hiking segments on the entire Lycian Way:

* Hikers starting from [Ovacık & Hisarönü](/en/articles/ovacik-hisaronu) walk ~13 km (4–5 hours) high along Babadağ's limestone flanks before dropping into Faralya.
* Continuing south out of Kabak, stone footpaths climb steeply toward tiny mountain hamlet **Alınca** with commanding sea panoramas.
* In calm morning seas, small boat rides or kayak trips link Kabak with tucked-away **Paradise Cove (*Cennet Koyu*)** and Pirate's Cove further south.

## Practical Driving, Cash & Budget Tips (2026)

1. **Drive Carefully on the Cliff Road:** The approach past Ölüdeniz and Kidrak features narrow blind bends directly alongside steep drops where minibuses and rental cars squeeze past each other. If unfamiliar with mountain switchbacks, aim to arrive before dusk rather than driving the pass after dark.
2. **Stock Up Before Leaving Fethiye or Ölüdeniz:** Faralya and Kabak carry only small village corner stores. Withdraw cash Turkish Lira, fill prescriptions, and pack high-SPF sunscreen or pharmacy essentials back in [Fethiye](/en/fethiye) or Ölüdeniz before heading up the ridge.
3. **Inflation Shield & Budget Spread:** Prices here span two completely opposite tiers—simple backpacker valley cabins and glamping camps (`€`) up to design-forward cliffside infinity-pool suites in Faralya (`€€–€€€`). Many valley retreats quote inclusive half-board packages; always confirm current rates for local 4×4 shuttles and camp dinners beforehand.

> [!TIP]
> **Smart Day-Trip Formula:** If you aren't overnighting in Kabak, arrive cleanly **before 11:00**. Park on the upper rim, hike down under morning shade while the sea stays calm and uncrowded, and grab lunch under the pine hammocks around 14:00 once afternoon waves roll in. Ride a local 4×4 shuttle back up to save your legs—and catch golden sunset light across Faralya's cliffs on your drive back toward Fethiye.
