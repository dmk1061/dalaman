# National Parks & Nature Reserves: Why This Coastline Stayed Green

Drive or sail anywhere between [Datça](/en/dacha) and [Kaş](/en/kas), and one thing stands out immediately compared with high-rise Mediterranean strips elsewhere: the shoreline stays shockingly green and low-slung. That isn't luck—it's law. Vast stretches of pine mountain, reed delta, and lagoon are locked under strict environmental zoning, from classic National Parks (*Milli Park*) and Nature Parks (*Tabiat Parkı*) to Turkey's flagship **Special Environmental Protection Areas (*Özel Çevre Koruma Bölgesi* — SEPA)**, where building codes often cap hotels at two stories.

For travelers, protection delivers crystal-clear coves, wild sea turtles, and quiet trails just half an hour from [Dalaman](/en/dalaman) Airport. Still, protected status brings real curfews, turnstiles, and hefty fines if you stumble across a rule unknowingly. Here is how the coast's key sanctuaries actually work.

## Dalyan–Köyceğiz SEPA & the DEKAMER Turtle Hospital

Stretching from freshwater [Lake Köyceğiz](/en/articles/koycegiz-lake) down the winding [Dalyan](/en/articles/dalyan-guide) reed beds to the 4.5-kilometer sandspit of [İztuzu Beach](/en/articles/iztuzu-beach), this reserve safeguards one of the Mediterranean's prime nesting strongholds for loggerhead sea turtles (**Caretta caretta**) alongside endangered freshwater **Nile softshell turtles (*Trionyx triunguis*)**.

* **Strict Night Curfew:** From May through October, İztuzu Beach shuts completely from **20:00 to 08:00**. Flashlights, fires, and late-night voices disorient nesting females hauling ashore and trick newborn hatchlings into crawling inland instead of out to sea. During the day, keep beach umbrellas well clear of the wooden stakes marking active nests, and note that jet skis and speedboats are barred from the cove.
* **DEKAMER Rescue Center:** At the eastern road-access end of İztuzu Beach sits **DEKAMER** (Sea Turtle Research, Rescue and Rehabilitation Center). Injured turtles—struck by boat propellers or tangled in fishing lines—recover here in saltwater tanks before their release back into the wild. Visitors can drop in daily (**10:00–18:00 in summer**, **10:00–16:00 in winter**); admission is **free**, though donations toward medicine and fish feed make a tangible difference.
* **Relict Sweetgum Groves:** Along the floodplains of Köyceğiz, Dalaman, and Günlüklü Bay survive rare, ancient groves of **oriental sweetgum (*Liquidambar orientalis*)**—a Tertiary-era endemic tree prized for aromatic storax resin whose wetland habitat is strictly protected against drainage.

## Ölüdeniz–Kıdrak Nature Park & the Fethiye–Göcek Marine Zone

That postcard sweep of the Blue Lagoon (Kumburnu Spit) and neighboring pine-shaded Kıdrak Cove sit inside the gated **Ölüdeniz–Kıdrak Nature Park (*Ölüdeniz–Kıdrak Tabiat Parkı*)**. Unlike Belcekız's open public beach outside the gate, entering Kumburnu or Kıdrak carries an official municipal/park tariff (**~150 ₺ / ~€3 per pedestrian**, or **~750 ₺ / ~€13–14 per standard car** in 2026; check current lira signage at the booth). Powered watercraft and jet skis are banned inside the lagoon, preserving its glassy calm.

Further northwest, the entire island-dotted bay between **[Göcek](/en/gocek)** and **[Fethiye](/en/fethiye)**—along with the sheer limestone canyon of **[Butterfly Valley](/en/articles/butterfly-valley)**, seasonal home to Jersey tiger moths (*Euplagia quadripunctaria*) and strictly off-limits to concrete development—lies within a marine SEPA. Boats overnighting in these coves must log regular pump-outs at shore stations via Turkey's mandatory **Mavi Kart** blackwater tracking system, backed by steep maritime fines.

## Marmaris National Park (*Marmaris Milli Parkı*)

Hugging the pine ridges ringing [Marmaris](/en/marmaris) down toward [İçmeler](/en/articles/icmeler), this park encompasses wild **Cennet (Nimara) Peninsula**, linked to the mainland by the narrow natural sandbar of Yalancı Boğaz ("False Strait"). Most of the headland is carpeted in fragrant **Turkish red pine (*Pinus brutia*)** that fills the morning air with warm resin.

A 400-to-500-meter forest trail climbs to the hilltop **Nimara Cave (*Nimara Mağarası*)**, where excavations uncovered 10,000-to-12,000-year-old cult offerings linked to the goddess Leto. Within park borders to the south also perch the cliffside theater ruins of ancient Carian **Amos** overlooking Kumlubük Bay.

## Saklıkent National Park & Protected Patara

East toward Kaş, two major reserves demand separate planning:

* **[Saklıkent Gorge](/en/articles/saklikent-gorge) National Park:** Protects the 18-kilometer limestone canyon and its snowmelt gorge. Entry runs **~250 ₺ (~€4.50)** per person in 2026—note that **MuseumPass cards are not valid** at national park gates.
* **Patara Special Protected Area:** Pairs Lycia's ancient league capital with 18 km of wind-sculpted sand dunes. Like İztuzu, Patara enforces a strict turtle curfew from **20:00 until dawn**, reserving the beach exclusively for nesting *Caretta caretta*.

> [!NOTE]
> **Critical Summer Alert (June–October): Forest Entry Bans (*Ormanlara Giriş Yasağı*).** During extreme summer heat and wildfire alerts, the Muğla Governor's Office and Forestry Directorate routinely issue temporary bans closing pine forests around Marmaris, Datça, and Fethiye to unauthorized hikers, along with absolute zero-tolerance bans on barbecues, open flames, and shisha charcoal outdoors. Before setting off on an independent pine-forest hike in July or August, check locally which coastal segments remain open and patrolled.

> [!TIP]
> **Insider Tip:** For a crowd-free wildlife morning, arrive at **DEKAMER** on İztuzu right around **10:00–11:00**, when volunteers feed recovering sea turtles and share their rescue stories, then wander west down the empty center stretch of the sandspit where sunbeds are strictly forbidden.
