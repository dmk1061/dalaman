# What to Drink Around Dalaman: From Rakı & Aegean Wines to Fresh Pomegranate

Secular Muğla Province mixes ancient drinking heritage with strict modern tax rules. Vineyards around [Datça](/en/dacha) and Kaunos pressed Aegean wine into clay amphorae two and a half millennia ago, and today independent brewers in nearby Muğla city bottle hop-forward craft ales. Yet punitive state excise duties (**ÖTV**) also make wine, beer, and spirits one of the fastest ways to inflate a holiday budget.

Beyond alcohol, Turkey's non-alcoholic cup culture shines at every hour—from copper-pot Turkish coffee and bracing black tea to wild pomegranate juice squeezed straight from [Dalaman](/en/dalaman) orchards.

## Buying Alcohol in 2026: Curfews, Stores & Sticker Shock

High special consumption taxes keep bottle prices closer to Scandinavia or the UK than Spain or Greece:

* **Where to Buy:** Look for independent bottle shops wearing blue-or-white **TEKEL** signs, or full-line supermarkets like **Migros**, **CarrefourSA**, and upscale **Macrocenter** (abundant in Göcek and Fethiye). Note that Turkey's omnipresent budget discounters (**BİM**, **A101**, **Şok**) **do not sell alcohol or cigarettes at all**.
* **The Strict 22:00–06:00 Retail Ban:** By national law, supermarkets and bottle shops **cannot ring up any alcohol between 22:00 and 06:00**—cash registers literally lock out the barcode after ten at night. Bars, restaurants, and *meyhanes* are exempt and serve legally until closing.
* **2026 Price Reality:** A 500 ml can or bottle of local beer at a supermarket averages **€1.50–2 (~85–110 ₺)**, while a solid bottle of Turkish wine starts around **€7–14**. At waterfront bars and *meyhanes*, expect **€6–10** per glass of house wine and **€9–15** for spirits or cocktails.

## *Rakı*: The Slow Art of "Lion's Milk"

Turkey's anise-infused national spirit (40–50% ABV), distilled from grapes and raisins, is never tossed back as a quick cold shot:

* **The Pouring Ritual:** Pour roughly one-third ***rakı*** into a narrow highball glass, top up with an equal share of chilled water—watching the clear liquid swirl magically cloud-white, earning its nickname ***Aslan sütü* ("Lion's Milk")**—and only *then* drop in one or two ice cubes (adding ice first shocks the anise oils into sharp floating crystals). Always keep a second glass of pure cold water alongside.
* **On the Table:** Sip *rakı* slowly over two or three unhurried hours strictly beside savory cold plates—brined white cheese (*beyaz peynir*), sweet sliced melon (*kavun*), garlic yogurt, and grilled seafood.
* **What to Order:** *Yeni Rakı* is the crisp benchmark everywhere; step up to triple-distilled or fresh-grape editions like **Tekirdağ Altın Seri** or green-bottled **Efe Yaş Üzüm** for a silkier, rounder finish.

## Aegean Wines & Indigenous Turkish Grapes

Skip imported supermarket bottles priced sky-high after tariffs and explore Turkey's own ancient varietals instead:

* **Crisp Whites:** **Narince** (floral stone-fruit balance), **Emir** (bright, volcanic minerality built for grilled sea bream), and Aegean **Sultaniye** (light, aromatic, and easy on a hot terrace).
* **Characterful Reds:** **Öküzgözü** ("bull's eye"—juicy dark berry fruit with lively acidity), **Boğazkere** (grippy, spiced, often blended seamlessly with Öküzgözü), and **Kalecik Karası** (silky, red-cherry elegance akin to Pinot Noir).
* **Trusted Labels & Vineyards:** Major producers **Kavaklıdere**, **Doluca**, **Pamukkale**, **Sevilen**, and **Urla** consistently deliver reliable bottles across coastal restaurants. Out exploring near Fethiye or on the [Datça Peninsula](/en/articles/datca-peninsula)? Seek out mountain-village boutiques around **[Yeşilüzümlü](/en/articles/yesiluzumlu-village)** or **Datça / Knidos Vineyard** estates—just stay cautious with overly sweet, unstable "homemade pomegranate or blackberry fruit wines" pushed at souvenir bus tastings.

## Beer & Muğla's Local Craft Gem: *Gara Guzu*

On draft or bottles, domestic lagers rule: crisp **Efes** (look out for smooth unpasteurized *Efes Özel Seri* or amber *Bomonti Filtresiz*) alongside clean Turkish-brewed **Tuborg** (pure malt lager brewed to German purity standards).

Want real hop flavor? Hunt down **Gara Guzu** ("Black Lamb"), an acclaimed independent craft microbrewery born right up the mountain road in provincial capital **Muğla**. Look for the cheeky black sheep label in Kaş, Fethiye Old Town bars, and bigger *Migros* fridges—their **Blonde Ale**, malty **Amber Ale**, and bright **Summer IPA** blow past generic resort pours.

## Coffee, Tea & Alcohol-Free Classics Around the Clock

* ***Çay* (Black Tea):** Rich Rize black tea double-steamed inside a stacked ***çaydanlık*** kettle and poured piping hot into tulip-waisted glass cups (*ince belli*) designed so your fingers grip the cooler rim.
* ***Türk Kahvesi* (Turkish Coffee):** Powder-fine grounds simmered slowly in a long-handled copper *cezve* pot and served unfiltered alongside a cool glass of water (sip water first to cleanse your palate). Always declare your sweetness level when ordering: ***sade*** (zero sugar), ***az şekerli*** (lightly sweet), ***orta*** (medium—most popular), or ***şekerli*** (sweet).
* ***Ayran* & *Şalgam*:** Chilled whisked salted yogurt (*ayran*) beats almost any sports drink in +36 °C heat. Curious eaters should also sample ***şalgam suyu***—tangy, salty fermented purple-carrot juice (ordered plain or spicy *acılı*) paired traditionally beside smoky Adana kebab.
* **Seasonal Squeeze & Winter *Salep*:** Autumn orchards circling **[Dalaman](/en/dalaman)** fuel sidewalk juice stands pressing dense ruby pomegranate juice (*nar suyu*), while misty November–March mornings bring cafés simmering velvety hot **salep**—spiced milk thickened with wild mountain orchid flour and dusted heavily with cinnamon.
