# Turunç & Ancient Amos: Bitter-Orange Gardens Behind Marmaris's Pine Pass

Leaving busy [Marmaris](/en/marmaris) behind for **Turunç** means climbing straight up over a steep pine ridge along tight corkscrew turns—and just 20 kilometers later, mega-hotels vanish abruptly into a sheltered horseshoe bay. In Turkish, *turunç* means **"bitter orange"** (Seville orange), and fragrant citrus orchards still tumble right behind small village guesthouses down to the seafront.

That sheer mountain wall is exactly what protected Turunç from high-rise package sprawl: expect quiet Blue Flag water, low-key waterfront taverns, and a free cliffside ancient Greek theater waiting an easy forest stroll from your beach towel.

## The Cove, Water & Quiet Village Tempo

Turunç's crescent stretch spans barely half a kilometer, yet geography turns every square meter into swimming bliss:

* **Calm Blue Flag Clarity:** Tight flanking headlands choke open Mediterranean swells down to gentle laps—keeping water crystal clear all day for easy shoreline snorkeling over rocky corners framing either bay tip.
* **Pebble Entry & Quick Depth:** Fine shoreline grit meets chunky pebbles underfoot (bring light aqua shoes) before sloping steadily deep alongside a neat flat promenade lined with family-run eateries and wooden piers ([compare coastal bays](/en/articles/beaches)).
* **Early-Night Peace:** Evenings wrap significantly quieter than neighboring **[İçmeler](/en/articles/icmeler)**—guests linger over apple or black tea on over-water timber decks, browse two sleepy lane shops, and turn in pleasantly early.

## Hidden Bonus Right Above Town: Free Ancient Amos (*Asarcık*)

Plenty of beachgoers sunbathe a full week oblivious that crowning the pine-dark southern bluff barely a hundred meters overhead rest the untouched classical ruins of **Amos**—one of Carian Rhodes's ancient mainland harbors ([explore more classical ruins](/en/articles/ancient-cities)):

1. **What Still Stands:** Ringed by olive trees and sweeping cliffs overlooking neighboring Kumlubük Bay sits an intimate **3rd–2nd-century BC Hellenistic theater** (classic semicircular Greek design facing open turquoise horizon!), sturdy polygonal citadel walls, the stone plinth of Apollo Samnios's temple, and subterranean cisterns.
2. **Zero Gates & Easy Trail Access:** **Entry stays 100% free** without booths or fences. Finest route up? Lace sneakers and follow the waymarked coastal cliff footpath (**Amos Mountain Track**) winding south out of Turunç in **35–45 pleasant minutes**—pack water! *(More headland loop tips live inside our [Lycian & Carian day hikes catalog](/en/articles/trekking-routes-lycian-coast). Driving instead? Follow tarmac 7–10 minutes toward Kumlubük and hike up ten easy minutes.)*

## Three Ways Into Turunç (Why Boat Wins Easily)

Getting over the ridge shapes your mood before you even swim:

* **Water Taxi From Marmaris or İçmeler (Top Pick May–October):** Wooden sea shuttles glide regularly straight off Marmaris and İçmeler piers directly onto Turunç's harbor dock (**~35–45 minutes**, around **€5–8 one-way**)—bypassing dizzy mountain switchbacks in exchange for salty sea breeze right to the sand.
* **Driving Over the Pine Pass:** Roughly 20 km (**30–35 minutes**) south from Marmaris center. Straight past İçmeler, asphalt climbs sharply beneath tall pines (pausing at summit pull-offs framing both bays) before plummeting down tight hairpins—take lower gears gently down grades if brakes run warm or passengers get carsick.
* **`MUTTAŞ` & Orange *Dolmuş* Minibuses:** Scheduled *Marmaris–Turunç* minibuses and municipal **MUTTAŞ** coaches tackle the pass daily—less frequent than İçmeler runs, so verify last evening departures (usually around **21:00–22:00** depending on month).

## Side Adventures, Shops & Best Months

* **Boat Coves & Cavern Dives:** Tiny wooden cruisers leave Turunç quay daily cruising quiet Bozburun headlands, while local scuba centers explore underwater overhangs beneath Amos cliffs ([coastal diving guide](/en/articles/diving)).
* **Push Further South to Çiftlik Bay:** Driving? Continue winding inland past Kumlubük and green village valleys toward sweeping dark-sand **[Çiftlik Bay](/en/articles/ciftlik-bay)**—pausing along ridge turnouts where morning light catches Greek Rhodes cleanly across the strait.
* **Daily Supplies & Market:** A cheerful small Monday produce market brings figs, homemade olive oil, and mountain honey into central Turunç; grocery needs clear easily at compact **ŞOK** and **Migros Jet** branches alongside currency booths and a daytime pharmacy (*Marmaris handles 24/7 overnight duty pharmacies—check live registries via [useful contacts](/en/articles/useful-contacts)*).

> [!TIP]
> **Insider Tip:** Because mountain walls trap afternoon heat in peak July–August, Turunç shines brightest in **May–June and September–mid-October**—just ~120 km (~2 hours including mountain passes) from [Dalaman Airport (`DLM`)](/en/articles/airport-dalaman). Claim a waterfront deck before **10:30** for Turkish breakfast in citrus shade before excursion boats from Marmaris fill the pier.
