# Events & Festivals Calendar Around Dalaman: Month by Month

Everyday coastal tempo between [Datça](/en/dacha) and [Kaş](/en/kas) swings to two overlapping clocks: sunlit seasonal festivals along the water and Turkey's shifting lunar holiday calendar. Midsummer brings open-air seaside jazz nights, yet travelers in the know swear coastal culture peaks in quiet shoulder months—when almond orchards burst pink in February, mountain villages feast on wild spring morels, and bright October skies turn kaleidoscopic over Ölüdeniz lagoon.

Here is the real **12-month coastal calendar**—no padded festivals borrowed from distant provinces—paired with crucial **2027 Bayram closure dates** so banks, highway passes, and full boutique hotels never ambush your plans.

## Five Dates Worth Planning Your Flight Around

* **February — Datça Almond Blossom Festival (*Datça Badem Çiçeği Festivali*):** While northern cities shiver under sleet, wild orchards across [Datça](/en/dacha) snow white and blush pink. Over a lively three-day weekend, Eski Datça and Palamutbükü swell with almond pastries, village honey stands, coastal walks, and traditional circles dancing under blossoms.
* **April — Yeşilüzümlü Morel Mushroom & *Dastar* Festival:** Set among mountain pines above Fethiye, villages honor prized wild spring morels (*kuzugöbeği mantarı*) paired with historic loom textiles from our [local crafts guide](/en/articles/suveniry-i-remesla). Square grills sizzle with mushroom skewers beside tastings from Fethiye's upland vineyards.
* **Late August — Kaş Jazz Festival (*Kaş Caz Festivali*):** Three sultry waterfront nights where saxophones carry across calm harbor ripples toward shadowed Meis island—easily the coast's sharpest late-summer acoustic weekend.
* **Mid-October — International Ölüdeniz Air Games:** Hundreds of elite freestyle flyers launch daily from Babadağ mountain ([paragliding guide](/en/articles/paragliding)), weaving choreographed smoke trails, tandem tricks, and wing-suit swoops directly above the Blue Lagoon.
* **Late October — Marmaris International Race Week & Republic Day (Oct 29):** Over a hundred sailing yachts close out Turkey's [yachting season](/en/articles/yachting) across vibrant marina regattas—just before Turkey explodes in red lanterns, torchlight parades, and waterfront fireworks honoring **Republic Day (*Cumhuriyet Bayramı*)** on October 29.

## Civic & Lunar Holidays: Real-World Road Impacts

Compare broad cultural holiday context in our [regional events overview](/en/articles/events-festivals), while pinning two crisp 2027 dates onto any travel spreadsheet:

* **2027 Islamic Lunar Holiday Windows (*Diyanet* Official Schedule):** Religious holidays shift 10–11 days earlier annually. In **2027**, three-day **Ramazan Bayramı (*Şeker Bayramı*)** falls on **March 9–11**, and the massive four-day **Kurban Bayramı** hits **May 16–19** (merging right into the May 19 Youth Day public holiday!).
* **What Actually Closes vs. Packs Out:** On major *Bayram* breaks and October 29, **banks, post offices (`PTT`), notaries, tax offices, and government immigration bureaus lock doors**—yet restaurants, cable cars, tour boats, supermarkets, and ancient sites stay open (with `muze.gov.tr` ruins opening around **13:00** on Day One of either *Bayram*). Domestic city drivers flood coastal highways down from Istanbul and Ankara: reserve cars and rooms at least one month prior and consult our [climate guide](/en/articles/climate-and-seasons) for calmer shoulder weeks.

> [!TIP]
> **November 10 at 09:05 Sharp:** On the anniversary of Mustafa Kemal Atatürk's passing, air-raid sirens sound across every Turkish city at **09:05 a.m.** Pedestrians freeze in their tracks, buses and cars halt instantly mid-intersection, and drivers step out onto the asphalt for one silent minute. Don't honk or shove lenses into people's faces—simply pause quietly with the street for sixty seconds of genuine respect.
