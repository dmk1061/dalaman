# Coastal Trekking Routes: Six Day Hikes From Datça to Kaş (Zero Tent Needed)

Hiking mountain pine ridges linking [Datça](/en/articles/datca-peninsula) and [Kaş](/en/articles/kas-guide) doesn't mean quitting your job for a month under an 18-kilogram pack. An intricate web of waymarked ancient mule paths loops cleanly into **single-day hikes** wrapping neatly by afternoon—ending with saltwater plunges and boat-taxi rides straight back to dinner.

Use this concrete route catalog alongside our broader [trekking preparation guide](/en/articles/trekking) and interactive **[coastal trekking route atlas](/en/routes/trekking)** to match distance, terrain, and transport cleanly.

## Two Great Long-Distance Networks: Lycian Way vs. Carian Trail

East from [Ovacık](/en/articles/ovacik-hisaronu) toward Antalya curls Turkey's famous **540+ km Lycian Way (*Likya Yolu*)**—while blanketing the entire wild west around Dalaman runs the newer, ruggedly peaceful **800–850 km Carian Trail (*Karya Yolu*)**.

Stretching red-and-white markers across empty olive terraces, pine ridges, and forgotten coastal spurs across **Datça** and **Bozburun** (from [Marmaris](/en/marmaris) clear out to **Knidos**), the Carian Trail sees roughly **five times fewer hikers** than headline Fethiye stages while serving equally jaw-dropping Aegean-Mediterranean cliff views.

## Two Hard Trail Rules: Where NOT to Take Chances

1. **NEVER Scramble Down Faralya Cliff Ropes Into Butterfly Valley:** Stale online posts still glamorize climbing down vertical knotted ropes off Faralya village into **[Butterfly Valley](/en/articles/butterfly-valley)**. Mountain rescue services warn bluntly: loose crumbling limestone ledges claim serious falls and fatalities almost yearly. Admire the cliff view safely from Faralya's road rim above, then catch an easy shuttle boat off Ölüdeniz boardwalk!
2. **Hike Strictly Spring or Autumn—Skip July & August:** Prime trekking sits solidly in **March–May** and **October–November** ([coastal weather guide](/en/articles/climate-and-seasons)). Midsummer heat turns open karst ridges past +42 °C inviting sudden heatstroke—plus between **June and late September**, Muğla authorities regularly enact emergency **wildfire forest-entry bans (*Orman Giriş Yasağı*)** policed with stiff Jandarma fines. Check trail clearance locally before setting out!

> [!TIP]
> **Minimum Day-Hiker Kit:** Even for brisk two-hour loops, pack **2–3 liters of water per walker** (summer ridge trails above Ölüdeniz and Datça run completely dry without fountains!), wear **lugged trail sneakers** gripping polished karst limestone (flip-flops twist ankles fast), and preload **Organic Maps** or **Wikiloc** offline GPX tracks—cell signal cuts dead inside coastal ravines.

## Reading Trail Markers & Solving One-Way Returns

* **GR Red-and-White Blazes:** Twin parallel **red and white** painted bars on boulders or pine trunks confirm you're squarely on course; a **red-and-white crossed "X"** signals *"wrong fork—turn back!"*, supplemented on open stony ridges by tiny stacked rock cairns.
* **Smart Point-A-to-Point-B Logistics:** Pair early morning **[Fethiye](/en/fethiye)** *dolmuş* drop-offs at Kayaköy with effortless seaside bus/taxi returns out of Ölüdeniz, or return smoothly by wooden water taxi from Limanağzı back into Kaş harbor. Tackling steep high passes across Babadağ? Arrange seasoned mountain guides via **[licensed local agencies](/en/articles/guides-and-travel-agencies)** or browse car-linked **[coastal weekend loops](/en/routes/weekends)**.
* **Basking Vipers & Shepherd Dogs:** Stay neatly on cleared stone treads away from dry tall summer weeds where timid vipers sunbathe ([regional fauna notes](/en/articles/flora-fauna)), pass rural livestock dogs calmly without waving sticks, and dial unified Turkish emergency rescue **`112`** from any mobile carrier if injured or lost.
