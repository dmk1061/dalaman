# Photo Guide From Datça to Kaş: Chasing Best Light & Turkish Drone Rules

Few coastlines hand photographers drama like the run between Datça and Kaş: pale limestone headlands dropping straight into neon turquoise coves, weathered rock tombs glowing gold above reed rivers, and mountain trails suspended high above anchored sails. Yet show up at midday at the wrong cove—or two hours early at a west-facing gorge—and dazzling turquoise collapses into flat white haze framed by forty selfie sticks.

Here are **nine signature camera spots** timed cleanly to the sun—followed by Turkey's unforgiving **500-gram drone customs law** every traveling pilot must read before packing bags.

## Five Light-Sensitive Classics: Exactly When to Arrive

1. **Belcekız Spit & Ölüdeniz Viewpoint ([Fethiye](/en/fethiye)):** Capture that famous sweeping aerial curve of the Blue Lagoon either from the pull-off bends along the mountain switchback descending from Hisarönü, or through gondola glass riding *Babadağ Teleferik* (1,200–1,700 m). **Best hour:** **before 09:30** before east-angled sun washes the cove milky, or the **last hour before sunset** to silhouette dozens of [paragliders](/en/articles/paragliding) drifting toward glowing horizons.
2. **Butterfly Valley Cliff Edge (Faralya):** Skip shooting upward from the pebble beach—the jaw-dropping frame opens from high on the cliff spur edging Faralya village. **Best hour:** **after 15:00–16:00**. Morning light hits from behind the ridge, sinking half the canyon into pitch shadow; late afternoon pours warm sun clear down to the shoreline surf. *(Critical safety note: zero guardrails line the crumbling karst rim—step carefully well back from loose limestone edges.)*
3. **Kaputaş Beach (Between Kalkan & [Kaş](/en/kas)):** Looking down the **187-step ravine** off the D400 bridge parapet frames pure emerald-and-cobalt gradient trapped between amber cliffs. **Best hour:** **11:00–14:30**. Outside midday, towering gorge walls plunge the sand into cold dark blue shadow. Shoot from the very top road railing rather than halfway down the concrete steps.
4. **İztuzu Sandbar & Delta Overlook ([Dalyan](/en/dalyan)):** Drive the winding spur up past the western beach turnoff toward the radar hill viewpoint (**İztuzu Seyir Tepesi**): from above, reed channels braid like emerald circuitry slamming into crisp Mediterranean surf. **Best hour:** golden hour straight into sunset, turning calm brackish lagoons to beaten silver.
5. **Kayaköy Ghost Village Upper Chapel:** Five hundred roofless stone cottages tumble photogenically down the hillside from the ridge beside the ruined upper church. **Best hour:** **16:30 onward** when low angled sunlight sets pale masonry burning honey-ochre and summer glare softens.

## Turkish Drone Customs Laws: Watch the 500-Gram Cliff

Before tucking a quadcopter into your carry-on, weigh it honestly with battery locked in. Turkey's Directorate General of Civil Aviation (**SHGM**) draws a razor-sharp customs boundary at **500 grams**:

* **Under 500 g (DJI Mini Series, etc.):** Micro-drones below 500 g escape mandatory online SHGM registration and typically breeze through airport X-rays as standard holiday electronics. Keep a printed factory spec sheet or saved screen clearly verifying takeoff weight (e.g., **249 g**) handy for curious officers.
* **500 g and Over (DJI Air, Mavic, Avata):** Treated legally as regulated aircraft requiring formal online import approval (**Form FR.03** technical compliance) plus prior flight clearance (**Form FR.23**). Here's the catch for short-stay tourists: **online SHGM registration links directly to Turkish citizenship/residency IDs (MERNİS)**. Bring an unregistered ≥500 g drone into Dalaman arrivals without pre-approved paperwork, and **airport customs routinely confiscate and impound the unit in bonded storage** until your departure flight home.

### No-Fly Zones Even for Sub-250 g Travel Drones

* **Ölüdeniz Lagoon & Mount Babadağ:** Dozens of tandem paragliders share tight airspace daily—flying any drone here risks mid-air canopy strikes and severe criminal liability.
* **Dalaman Airport (`DLM`) 9 km Approach Cone & Military Zones:** Never power props anywhere near runway paths or beside Turkey's major **Aksaz Naval Base** near [Marmaris](/en/marmaris), police stations, or coastal Jandarma compounds.
* **Ticketed Archaeological Sites & National Parks:** Inside state ruins ([Tlos, Patara, Kaunos, Xanthos](/en/articles/ancient-cities)) and protected reserves, aerial filming requires written Ministry of Culture permission—site guards spot buzzing rotors fast and order immediate landings and card wipes.
* **Altitude & Visual Contact:** Stay strictly under **120 meters AGL** and inside unaided **Visual Line of Sight (VLOS)**—ignoring airspace rules carries heavy fines under local [safety regulations](/en/articles/useful-tips).
