# Underwater Turquoise Coast: Diving in Kaş, Fethiye & Marmaris

Comparing southwest Turkey to the Red Sea misses the point. You won't find neon barrier reefs between [Datça](/en/dacha) and [Kaş](/en/kas)—instead, experienced divers return year after year for dramatic **karst limestone architecture**. The same white rock that sculpts Lycian cliffs plunges straight down underwater into sheer drop-offs, sunlit caverns, swim-through arches, and sandy shelves littered with Byzantine amphorae, antique stone anchors, and thoughtfully scuttled wrecks.

Because most coves here sit over pebble and rock rather than churned silt, visibility off Kaş and the outer bays regularly pushes **30 to 40 meters**, while loggerhead sea turtles (*Caretta caretta*) cruise calmly above the reefs from May right into November.

## Kaş: Turkey's Undisputed Diving Capital

Roughly two and a half hours east of [Dalaman](/en/dalaman) Airport, bohemian **[Kaş](/en/kas)** wakes up each morning to a waterfront bristling with timber dive boats. Over **30 moored dive sites** lie 10 to 30 minutes out from the harbor, spanning beginner sand gardens to deep technical walls:

* **Douglas C-47 Dakota Wreck:** Kaş's headline magnet—a scuttled military transport plane with a **29-meter wingspan** resting upright on pale sand at roughly **22 meters** (requires *Advanced Open Water Diver* certification).
* **The Canyon & *Dimitri* Wreck:** Twin limestone cliffs descending side by side underwater before tumbling past a sunken cargo ship—hypnotic shafts of blue light, suited to confident certified divers comfortable with depth and occasional current.
* **Flying Fish (*Uçanbalık*):** An exposed offshore reef dropping past the mangled remains of a WWII Italian bomber (**55–70 meters**; technical/trimix territory).
* **Easy Shore & Cove Sites:** Sheltered **Limanağzı** and **Hidayet Bay** (**6–16 meters**) teem with schooling barracuda, dusky groupers, camouflaged octopuses, and grazing sea turtles—perfect for first breaths underwater.

## Fethiye & Marmaris: Caves & Calm Starter Bays

Short on time and staying closer to [Dalaman](/en/dalaman)? Both western bays offer calm conditions for DSD trials or earning your *PADI / CMAS Open Water* card:

* **[Fethiye](/en/fethiye) & Ölüdeniz:** Flagship sites include the dramatic semi-submerged cavern of **Afkule**, *Hamam* ("Turkish Bath"), and **Three Tunnels (*Üç Tüneller*)**, while sheltered **Dalyan Bay** (near Fethiye Marina) and **Aquarium Cove** give beginners calm sandy bottoms.
* **[Marmaris](/en/marmaris):** Over 50 charted sites stretch toward İçmeler and Bozburun—ranging from beginner-friendly **Abdi Reis (Aquarium)** to **Baca Cave (*Baca Mağarası*)**, where midday shafts of sunlight pierce straight down through the cavern roof, plus scattered ancient amphora fields off **Serçe** and **Kadırga Cape**.

## Top Snorkeling Spots (Mask & Fins Only)

* **Limanağzı & Kaş Coves:** Sea turtles regularly graze on seagrass beds right below the wooden jetties—arrive in the morning before day boats fill the bay.
* **Datça Aquarium Cove & Bozburun Gebekse Bay:** White pebble-and-limestone seabeds with zero mud make fish and sponges pop vividly even from the surface.
* **12 Islands (Fethiye–Göcek Bay):** Easy stops at Yassıca Islands, Aquarium Bay, and Kapıdağ Peninsula on any standard day cruise.

## Legal Reality Check: Spearfishing in Turkey

> [!NOTE]
> **Strict Turkish Spearfishing Laws (*Tebliğ № 6/2*, 2024–2028):** **Spearfishing on SCUBA tanks is strictly illegal nationwide.** Even free-dive breath-hold spearfishing (*zıpkın*) now requires a valid endorsed amateur fishing permit (**Amatör Balıkçı Belgesi**, mandatory across coastal inspections from January 2026—virtually impossible to obtain seamlessly as a short-stay foreign tourist without residency registration) and remains **100% prohibited inside marine protected zones, harbors, the entire Fethiye–Göcek SEPA, and Kekova**. Violation means heavy fines and immediate gear confiscation. Stick to licensed dive boats or guided rod-and-line boat charters instead.

> [!TIP]
> **Insider Tip:** Peak underwater clarity and warmest comfort hit in **late May–June** and **September–October** (October water stays a balmy +24 °C while boat decks feel blissfully uncrowded). Before paying any deposit, check that the boat carries valid **TSSF** (*Turkish Underwater Sports Federation*) credentials—and never forget the **18-to-24-hour surface interval before flying out of Dalaman Airport (`DLM`)**.
