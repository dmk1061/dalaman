# Ölüdeniz Guide: Two Very Different Seas Divided by One Pine Spit

**Ölüdeniz** (literally *"Dead Sea"*) anchors nearly every postcard of southwest Turkey—yet stepping off the *dolmuş* in person surprises almost everyone. Rather than one unified resort beach, the bay splits abruptly down a narrow sandy spit into **two completely opposite seas** ten paces apart.

On one side of the stone promenade rolls lively, sparkling turquoise open-sea surf where colorful paragliders flare onto paving stones every two minutes. Behind the pine headland lies the enclosed glass basin of the **Blue Lagoon**, flat as bathwater even on gusty afternoons. Knowing which side fits your day—and how to dodge parking gridlock—makes all the difference.

### Honest Swim Note About the Blue Lagoon

Aerial brochure shots over Kumburnu look unreal from Mount Babadağ, yet down at eye level inside the shallowest inner bend of the lagoon on peak July–August afternoons, crowds pile thick and unmoving water warms past +29–30 °C. Want cool clarity *and* room to stretch? Walk out to the **outer tip of Kumburnu Spit** facing open bay currents, or claim coarse pebble outside at **Belcekız before noon** (slip on rubber aqua shoes to save soles). Compare every bay along the coast in our [beaches overview](/en/articles/beaches).

## What to Do Off the Towel

1. **Watch Non-Stop Paraglider Landings:** Even if skipping heights yourself, grab morning espresso or sunset beers along Belcekız boardwalk around **11:00 or 17:30**. Every two minutes tandem canopies swoop gracefully off [Mount Babadağ](/en/articles/babadag) straight onto cushioned landing targets steps away—free live coastal theater (weight limits and camera pricing sit inside our [paragliding guide](/en/articles/paragliding)).
2. **Board Morning Shuttles to Butterfly Valley & St. Nicholas Island:** Ticket desks along Belcekız beach launch wooden 6-Island day galleons (**10:00–11:00**) alongside brisk water-shuttle launches straight to **[Butterfly Valley](/en/articles/butterfly-valley)** (20–30 minutes) and Byzantine **[St. Nicholas Island (Gemiler)](/en/articles/st-nicholas-island)**.
3. **Hike the Pine Ridge to Kayaköy (6 km):** Slip past the west lagoon parking lane up a clearly blazed rocky footpath climbing 6 km through scented pinewoods straight to abandoned **[Kayaköy Ghost Village](/en/articles/kayakoy-ghost-town)**—go early with water, eat lunch under orange groves, and ride a *dolmuş* home. Just uphill in **[Ovacık](/en/articles/ovacik-hisaronu)** starts the official 540-kilometer **Lycian Way** ([coastal trekking guide](/en/articles/trekking)).

## Getting Here & Dodging Parking Snags

* **Taking the *Dolmuş*:** From [Fethiye](/en/fethiye) bus terminal and downtown mosque square, white **Ölüdeniz** minibuses depart every **10–15 minutes** down through Ovacık and Hisarönü (**~25 minutes**, ~€1.50–2, running well toward midnight in summer).
* **The Dead-End Car Trap:** Driving a [rental car](/en/articles/vehicle-rental-cars-scooters-bikes)? **Arrive before 09:30.** The steep resort avenue plunging past [Ovacık and Hisarönü](/en/articles/ovacik-hisaronu) funnels straight into a seafront dead end where free curbside pockets clog solid by 10:30, leaving pricey €3–5 private lots or standstill gridlock. Better option after 10:00: turn cleanly through **Kumburnu Park gate** for guaranteed shaded parking beneath pines, or continue 3 km south to spacious **Kıdrak Cove**.

## Seasonality, Evenings & Base Strategy

Unlike twelve-month port towns, Ölüdeniz swings sharply with holiday calendars (verify exact weather inside our [climate and seasons guide](/en/articles/climate-and-seasons)):

* **High Summer (July–August):** Belcekız fills towel-to-towel by 10:30, beachfront [hotels and resorts](/en/articles/accommodation) hit annual ceiling rates, and waterfront strip lanes bake warmly past dinner. Best local swims fall **07:30–10:00** or post-17:30.
* **Sweet-Spot Shoulder (May–June & September–October):** Peak magic—glassy lagoon water stays +23–25 °C well into late October while breezes mellow, capped mid-October by the soaring International Air Games ([events calendar](/en/articles/events-and-festivals-calendar)).
* **Winter Quiet (November–March):** Nearly every boardwalk bar and ticket booth shutters completely, handing an empty, silent shoreline back to expats walking with flasks of hot tea.

> **Insider Tip:** Beachfront hotels in Ölüdeniz carry hefty nightly rates plus boat-generator hum and bar bass until midnight. For cooler +3–4 °C breezes, leafy pools, and sharper budgets, stay five minutes uphill in green **[Ovacık](/en/articles/ovacik-hisaronu)** and hop the frequent *dolmuş* straight to sand in seven minutes—or arrange stress-free [Dalaman airport transfers](/en/services/transfers) right to your villa gate.
