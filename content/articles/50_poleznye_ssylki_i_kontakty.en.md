## 24/7 Emergency & Traveler Helplines

Turkey eliminated separate fire, ambulance, and police hotlines years ago (legacy three-digit codes forward automatically, though dialing direct saves precious seconds):

* **`112` — Unified Emergency Dispatch (*Acil Çağrı Merkezi*):** One free call connects **ambulance, city police, rural Jandarma (highways, villages, beaches), fire brigade, Coast Guard, and mountain rescue**. English-speaking dispatchers work continuously across Muğla and Antalya provinces (for insurance and clinic guidance, review our [safety guide](/en/articles/useful-tips)).
* **`Alo 176` (from overseas or roaming: `+90 312 176 11 22`) — Ministry of Tourism Helpline:** Multilingual support (English, German, Turkish, Russian) answering museum questions and logging formal complaints over unlicensed operators or tourist overcharging.
* **`YİMER 157` (from overseas/roaming: `+90 312 157 11 22`) — Foreigners Communication Center:** 24/7 official migration guidance covering visas, [İkamet residency](/en/articles/residency-permit), plus an urgent distress option (**press `1`**) for life-safety or maritime incidents.

## Finding 24/7 Night & Sunday Pharmacies (*Nöbetçi Eczane*)

Standard neighborhood pharmacies (*Eczane*) open Monday through Saturday roughly **08:30–19:00**, pulling shutters down strictly overnight and all day **Sunday** plus public holidays. Outside those hours, each district appoints rotating overnight duty pharmacies (**Nöbetçi Eczane**) staffed right through until **08:30** next morning (look for a lit doorway buzzer to wake the duty pharmacist).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Always Check Today's Duty Pharmacy via Official Databases Only:**
> 1. **Muğla Chamber of Pharmacists (*Muğla Eczacı Odası*):** `https://www.muglaeo.org.tr/nobetci-eczaneler/` — pick your exact town (*Dalaman*, *Ortaca*, *Fethiye*, *Marmaris*, *Datça*, *Köyceğiz*, etc.) for live street addresses and GPS map pins. *(For **[Kaş](/en/kas)**, which sits just across the border inside Antalya Province, use `e-Devlet` below or Antalya Chamber of Pharmacists at `https://www.antalyaeo.org.tr`).*
> 2. **National `e-Devlet` Pharmacy Search (All of Turkey incl. Kaş):** `https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/saglik-titck-nobetci-eczane-arama` — Turkey's countrywide Health Ministry lookup filtered by province, district, and date.
> 3. **On the Street:** Walk up to the dark glass door of *any* closed pharmacy nearby—by law, every *Eczane* posts an illuminated board showing tonight's exact active *Nöbetçi Eczane* addresses and phone numbers.
