# Diving, Tuna Trolling & Spearfishing Off Lycia: What's Legal in 2026

Between [Marmaris](/en/marmaris), [Fethiye](/en/fethiye), and [Kaş](/en/kas), calm summer water pushes visibility neatly past 25–35 meters. Steep limestone shelves plunge into submerged grottoes, fields of intact Byzantine amphorae, and dramatic shipwrecks. Yet while recreational diving follows familiar international routines, street flyers pitching *"year-round deep-sea Bluefin tuna"* or *"SCUBA spearfish hunts"* push travelers straight toward blatant scams—or stiff maritime prosecution.

Here is where coastal diving actually sparkles alongside Turkey's exact **2026 fishing and spearfishing laws**, where violations risk gear confiscation and vessel seizure rather than polite warnings.

## 1. Recreational Diving: Three Signature Coastal Hubs

If you value warm +25–28 °C summer water, crystalline visibility, and sheltered topography over soft coral walls, southwest Turkey delivers effortlessly. Compare dive operators and prices in our full [diving guide](/en/articles/diving) or browse bookable [diving charters](/en/services/diving):

* **[Kaş](/en/kas) — Turkey's Unrivaled Underwater Capital:** Over 30 moored sites lie 15–25 minutes off Kaş quay: the iconic 29-meter **Douglas C-47 Dakota** transport aircraft resting upright at ~22 m (AOWD required), **The Canyon** plunging past 30 m alongside the sunken *Dimitri* freighter bow, deep **Flying Fish (*Uçanbalık*)**, a scuttled military tank, and scattered gardens of untouched classical amphorae (strictly look-don't-touch!). Loggerhead turtles (*Caretta caretta*) and curious dusky groupers cross paths with divers almost daily.
* **[Fethiye](/en/fethiye) — Gentle Starter Bays & *Afkule* Cavern:** Sheltered **Hamam Bay** beside Kızılada shields first-time *Intro / DSD* divers across calm 5–8 meter slopes, while certified explorers venture to **Afkule**'s dramatic limestone cliff wall where sunlight pierces inside a semi-submerged grotto.
* **[Marmaris](/en/marmaris) & [Datça](/en/dacha) — Reefs & Amphora Ridges:** More than 50 charted rock arches and wall dives stretch from Kumlubük Cape toward Datça—far fewer boats crowding anchors than Kaş, with abundant moray eels, octopuses, and patrolling barracuda schools.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Beware Off-Season "Bluefin Tuna" Claims:** Aligned with international **ICCAT** conservation treaties, Turkey enforces a strict closed season banning recreational Bluefin tuna fishing from **October 15 through June 15**. Any promenade flyer pitching *"giant Bluefin hunting"* in April, May, or November is either bait-and-switching you onto light bonito trolling—or running illegal poaching watched sharply by Turkish Coast Guard patrol boats (*Sahil Güvenlik*).

## 3. Spearfishing (*Zıpkın*): Tight 2026 Rules Under *Tebliğ № 6/2*

Still spotting sketchy listings online advertising *"SCUBA spearfishing trips"*? Walk away immediately. Under Turkey's active ministerial circular **Tebliğ № 6/2 (2024–2028)**, strict rules govern every single speargun entering coastal waters:

1. **Zero SCUBA Tanks—Breath-Hold Daylight Only:** Recreational spearfishing (*Sualtı Avcılığı*) is **strictly legal ONLY from sunrise to sunset on natural breath-hold freediving**. Pairing a speargun with compressed air tanks, hookah hoses, or underwater night torches is instant poaching—triggering heavy statutory fines and confiscation of dive gear.
2. **Mandatory Endorsed Permit Starting January 1, 2026:** From January 2026 onward, speargun divers must hold an officially endorsed Turkish amateur angler license (**Amatör Balıkçı Belgesi** specifically stamped for spearfishing via *Tarım ve Orman Müdürlüğü*—impractical for short-stay holidaymakers without local residency). Selling recreational catch to restaurants or markets is illegal.
3. **Total Bans on Protected Species & Antiquities Zones:** Harpooning Bluefin tuna without individual Ministry quota and electronic **e-BCD** clearance is strictly unlawful; year-round zero-tolerance bans likewise protect sea turtles, Mediterranean monk seals, threatened groupers (*lahos/orfoz* in restricted areas), plus all submerged archaeological sites including **Kekova** and **Gemiler Bay**.
