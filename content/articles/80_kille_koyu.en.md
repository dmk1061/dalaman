# Kille Bay (*Kille Koyu*): Natural Pine Canopy & Forest Ruins Off Göcek

Tucked deep inside a quiet fold of **[Göcek Bay](/en/gocek)** rests wild horseshoe **Kille Bay (*Kille Koyu* / *Kille Bükü*)**—curing coastal Turkey's sharpest midday annoyance in one stroke: **you barely need a sun umbrella here**. Towering hundred-year-old Turkish red pines spill down rocky hills right onto pebble surf, casting dense mottled green woodland shade even at blistering July noon.

Step ten paces back beneath those fragrant branches and tumbled Byzantine stone church walls emerge quietly from forest ferns—a rare Gulf of Göcek yachting sanctuary ordinary travelers can also reach cleanly by car down a quiet woodland gravel track.

## Four Reasons Travelers Seek Out Kille Bay

1. **Built-In Tree Canopy & Cool Freshwater Springs:** Broad pebble shoreline (medium-to-coarse smooth gray-white stones—slip on **aqua shoes!**) stays fringed continuously by mature pines, while cool mountain springs trickling softly off coastal hills keep surface bay water crisp and refreshing even when open coves simmer warm.
2. **Byzantine Chapel Ruins Behind the Trees:** Unlike purely boat-only yacht coves across the gulf, Kille pairs sea swims with peaceful shore heritage—weathered vaulted arches and rough masonry blocks of an **old Byzantine coastal church** hide directly behind shoreline pines ([national parks & wild heritage](/en/articles/national-parks-and-nature-reserves)).
3. **Generous Breathing Space:** Even when wooden 12-Island gulets glide in midday for anchor lunches, Kille's sweeping crescent still leaves ample hushed pine shade clear along quiet gravel wings.
4. **Deceptively Close to Hubs:** Perched between **[Dalaman](/en/dalaman)** and Göcek—roughly **35–40 minutes** from [Dalaman Airport (`DLM`)](/en/articles/airport-dalaman) and just under an hour west of [Fethiye](/en/fethiye).

## Reaching Kille: Forest Track vs. Coming Under Sail

Getting ashore weeds out lazy resorts cleanly:

* **Driving In (Exact Route):** Along the paved **Göcek–Dalaman** highway roughly near the **13 km** pine stretch, spot turnoff signage pointing down toward **Kille Koyu**. Leave highway asphalt onto roughly **7 kilometers of graded stabilized forest dirt-and-gravel road** winding peacefully through trees. Any normal non-4x4 hatchback clears it comfortably May–October provided you creep gently round rainwater gullies and protruding stones; expect modest seasonal forestry gate entry fees during busy months.
* **Sailing Aboard 12-Island Cruises:** Kille doubles as a staple calm swim stop aboard wooden cruisers departing **[Göcek harbor](/en/articles/gocek-guide)** and Fethiye ([yachting overview](/en/articles/yachting)), normally dropping midday anchor around lunch.

## CRITICAL Summer Law: ZERO Barbecues, Stoves or Open Flames!

Ignore reckless outdated blog posts cheerfully telling visitors *"pack portable BBQs into Kille Bay for picnics under pines!"* Doing that today invites steep fines or immediate arrest:

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Zero-Fire Law Across Muğla Forests (June–October):** Sun-baked pine needles turn Southwest Turkish headlands into tinderboxes every summer. Under strict active orders from the Muğla Governor's Office and Forestry Directorate (OGM), **ALL campfires, charcoal barbecues, portable camping gas stoves, and discarded cigarette butts inside forested bays like Kille are STRICTLY ILLEGAL** ([eco-tourism rules](/en/articles/eco-tourism-and-volunteering)). During red-alert fire winds, rural Jandarma patrols periodically chain off forest access gates completely! Bring cold sandwiches, fresh fruit, chilled water thermoses—and **pack 100% of wrappers cleanly back to town bins**.

> [!TIP]
> **Pack Self-Sufficiently Before Turning Off Highway:** Kille features zero guaranteed cafés, shops, or working year-round kiosks—occasional modest summer stalls close capriciously, ATMs don't exist, and phone signal drops dead! Load folding mats or light chairs, 2+ liters of water per traveler, snorkel masks, closed sneakers for wandering Byzantine chapel stones safely, and pebble water shoes ([compare nearby Sarsala Bay](/en/articles/sarsala-bay)).

## Best Times & Who Fits Kille Best

Forget full-service beach clubs delivering mojitos to cushioned loungers—Kille charms hikers, sea-kayakers, and families weary of clattering waterfront promos:

* **Sweetest Hours by Car:** Roll down between **08:30 and 11:30**—or **after 16:00** once daytime excursion boats clear the cove for Göcek marinas, leaving calm translucent water lit softly under quiet red pines.
* **Check Track Surface After Winter Rains:** Heavy December–March storms occasionally carve fresh ruts across those 7 gravel kilometers; stick cleanly to smooth crown tracks in early spring or skip driving altogether by joining calm **[day boat tours from Göcek](/en/services/yacht-rental)** instead.
