# İztuzu Beach: Five Kilometers of Turtle-Guarded Gold Between River & Sea

Across a rocky southwest coast dominated by pebbles and cliff ladders, **[Dalyan](/en/dalyan)'s İztuzu Beach** feels almost surreal: a slender **4.5-to-5-kilometer golden sandspit** forming a natural dam where salty Mediterranean surf breaks yards from warm, reed-lined freshwater channels of the Dalyan River delta.

Back in the late 1980s, developers poured foundations to plant mega-resorts squarely atop these dunes until a fierce international campaign led by British conservationist **June Haimoff** (*"Kaptan June"* to admiring locals) forced Turkey to cancel construction forever. Today İztuzu stands protected as one of the Mediterranean's paramount nesting sanctuaries for endangered loggerhead sea turtles (*Caretta caretta*).

## Two Waters Separated by Thirty Paces of Sand

Walk barely thirty yards across warm dunes, and two completely opposite ecosystems frame your towel:

* **The Mediterranean Sea Side:** Rare soft, stone-free fine golden sand shelving so gently into clear surf that toddlers wade thirty meters safely without sharp drop-offs ([compare top coastal beaches](/en/articles/beaches)).
* **The Calm River Side:** Behind low scrub dunes lies the placid jade brackish river basin—warmer, waveless, and occasionally frequented right alongside timber pilings by giant aquatic **Nile softshell turtles (*Trionyx triunguis*)** cruising silently for crabs.

## Four Unbreakable Rules Protecting Nesting *Caretta caretta*

Every summer between **May and September**, heavy 100-kilogram females crawl out of dark surf to bury eggs before silver-lit hatchlings sprint instinctively toward moonlit sea reflection weeks later. Respecting İztuzu's strict code protects both turtles and your wallet from hefty environmental fines ([see our wildlife and conservation guides](/en/articles/eco-tourism-and-volunteering)):

1. **Strict Nocturnal Curfew (20:00 – 08:00):** Throughout nesting months (typically May 1 into late September/October), beaches lock dead empty from **20:00 until dawn**. Zero camping, zero beach bonfires, and zero flashlights or camera strobes—artificial glare ruins nesting mothers and lures hatchlings inland to perish.
2. **Keep Umbrellas Tight to the Wet Tide Line:** During daylight hours, stake personal beach parasols solely down on open wet sand nearest surf—never deeper up warm dry sand where protective wire pyramids guard delicate egg clutches half a meter below.
3. **Zero Pets, Jet Skis or Loud Parties:** Dogs stay strictly barred off the spit, and motorized watercraft (jet skis, banana boats) remain banned offshore.
4. **Visit the DEKAMER Sea Turtle Hospital:** Right behind the southern road entrance sits **DEKAMER**, Turkey's active turtle rescue clinic. Admission stays free (donations warmly keep tanks running): meet injured adult loggerheads healing from propeller gashes or swallowed hooks before their release back to sea ([explore more coastal fauna](/en/articles/flora-fauna)).

## Two Ways to Arrive (Picking Your Side of the Spit)

Basic municipal amenities—clean showers, changing cabins, inexpensive municipal snacks, and fair-priced sunbeds—cluster strictly at **opposite ends of the spit**, protecting three untouched center kilometers purely as raw dunes ([full Dalyan town guide](/en/articles/dalyan-guide)):

* **North End ("The Estuary Mouth") by Cooperative Riverboat:** From Dalyan's downtown waterfront pier, wooden cooperative motor launches depart roughly every 30 minutes gliding **35–45 minutes** through tall golden reed mazes beneath ancient cliff tombs right to İztuzu's northern timber jetty (~€4–7 return; check final boats around **18:00–18:30**!).
* **South End ("DEKAMER Side") by Car or *Dolmuş*:** Follow 12 smooth paved minutes winding past mirror-still Lake Sülüngür and pine hills directly to the southern gatehouse. Stop at cliff turnout **İztuzu Seyir Tepesi** halfway up the ridge for the coast's classic emerald-delta panorama ([photo timing guide](/en/articles/photo-guide-best-instagram-spots)). Cars pay a modest municipal eco-gate toll (~€2–3), while town minibuses stop steps from DEKAMER's entrance.

> [!TIP]
> **Insider Tip:** Between **12:00 and 15:30**, excursion cruisers hauling groups from Marmaris and Fethiye fill both outer tips loudly. Simply grab your water bottle, hat, and sunblock (zero trees shade mid-spit sand!) and **walk 400–500 meters into the untouched center section**: within ten minutes, umbrellas vanish behind you, leaving three quiet kilometers of warm sand and clean waves nearly to yourself. Driving back by [rental car](/en/services/car-rental) right around sunset curfew (~19:00)? Pack a light jumper—evening reed breezes along Sülüngür Lagoon cool fast at dusk.

## Day-Bag Checklist Before Leaving Town

* **1.5–2 liters of drinking water, wide-brim hat & SPF 50:** Zero shade kiosks or supermarkets line central sand.
* **Small Cash Lira Notes:** Municipal cafés and lounger booths rent umbrellas cheaply (~€3–5), yet sparse coastal cellular reception occasionally stalls mobile card machines.
* **Simple Flip-Flops:** Soft pebble-free sand needs zero rock shoes—you'll only want sandals crossing scorching noon sand between cold showers and the surf line!
