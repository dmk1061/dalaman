# Events, Festivals & Public Holidays: Timing Your Coast Trip Right

The cultural calendar between [Dalaman](/en/dalaman), [Datça](/en/dacha), and [Kaş](/en/kas) completely transforms quiet coastal rhythms outside mid-summer. February turns the Datça Peninsula pink under almond blossoms; April draws mushroom foragers into pine villages; October fills Ölüdeniz's sky with acrobatic gliders while a hundred spinnakers race across Göcek and Marmaris bays.

Just as crucial for trip logistics is tracking Turkey's **national and religious holidays**. Knowing when they hit helps you join colorful waterfront torchlight parades—or avoid paying double for rental cars and domestic Istanbul flights during four-day national gridlock.

## National Public Holidays (Banks, Notaries & Government Offices Closed)

On these fixed calendar dates, city halls, banks, *Göç İdaresi* immigration desks, post offices, and notaries close nationwide while red crescent-and-star banners drape every ferry and town square:

* **January 1:** New Year's Day (*Yılbaşı*)
* **April 23:** National Sovereignty & Children's Day (*Ulusal Egemenlik ve Çocuk Bayramı*)
* **May 1:** Labor & Solidarity Day (*Emek ve Dayanışma Günü*)
* **May 19:** Commemoration of Atatürk, Youth & Sports Day (*Atatürk'ü Anma, Gençlik ve Spor Bayramı*)
* **July 15:** Democracy & National Unity Day (*Demokrasi ve Millî Birlik Günü*)
* **August 30:** Victory Day (*Zafer Bayramı*)
* **October 29:** Republic Day (*Cumhuriyet Bayramı*) — Turkey's grandest civic celebration, capped by harborfront concerts, torch processions, and fireworks across Fethiye, Marmaris, and Kaş.

## Religious Holidays: Crucial Travel Planning for 2026–2027

Islamic holidays follow the lunar calendar, shifting backward by **10–11 days every year**:

* **The Month of Ramadan (*Ramazan*):** Along secular coastal towns in Muğla and Antalya, visitors feel almost zero disruption—beaches, ferry crossings, cafes, and bars run completely normally, while municipal squares glow after dusk with lively evening craft fairs and communal *iftar* fast-breaking dinners.
* **Ramazan Bayramı / Şeker Bayramı (Eid al-Fitr):** A 3-day celebration marking Ramadan's end. In **2027**, the official holiday falls on **March 9–11** (with a half-day *Arefe* eve on March 8).
* **Kurban Bayramı (Eid al-Adha):** Turkey's biggest 4-day holiday window. In **2027**, dates fall on **May 16–19**—stacking directly onto the **May 19** Youth Day holiday to create a massive domestic long week.

During both *Bayram* breaks, millions drive south from Istanbul, Ankara, and Izmir. Domestic flights into `DLM`, coastal boutique hotels, and economy car rentals jump dramatically in price or sell out weeks out—and expect many family-run bakeries and backstreet *lokantas* to pull shutters down until lunchtime on Day One.

## Six Signature Festivals From Datça to Kaş

* **February — Datça Almond Blossom Festival (*Badem Çiçeği Festivali*):** While northern Europe scrapes windshields, the [Datça Peninsula](/en/articles/datca-peninsula) bursts into white-and-blush clouds of flowering orchards, hosting folk markets in Eski Datça, almond sweet stalls, coastal walks, and open-air music.
* **April — Yeşilüzümlü Morel Mushroom Festival (*Kuzugöbeği Mantar Festivali*):** Perched in pine hills above Fethiye, [Yeşilüzümlü](/en/articles/yesiluzumlu-village) hosts guided forest mushroom hunts, local boutique wine tastings, and demonstrations of heirloom wooden-loom *dastar* weaving.
* **June — Kaş Culture & Arts Festival (*Kaş Kültür Sanat Festivali*):** Evening concerts staged across harbor squares and the stone steps of Kaş's sea-facing ancient theater beneath summer stars.
* **Late June to July — Dalyan Turtle Awareness Days:** Eco-walks, beach talks, and waterfront concerts celebrating peak *Caretta caretta* nesting season around [Dalyan](/en/dalyan) and İztuzu.
* **October — International Ölüdeniz Air Games (*Ölüdeniz Hava Oyunları Festivali*):** One of the Mediterranean's greatest free sky spectacles. For a full week, hundreds of international freestyle pilots paint the sky over [Ölüdeniz](/en/articles/oludeniz) and Mount Babadağ with synchronized wing formations, BASE jumps, and costumed tandem flights.
* **Late October to November — Autumn Yacht Regattas:** Over a hundred international racing crews close out sailing season during **Marmaris International Race Week** alongside Göcek's crisp autumn regattas—filling marina boardwalks with live music and sailmakers' energy.

> [!TIP]
> **Insider Tip:** Eyeing coastal dates in **mid-May 2027 (May 15–23)**? Lock in boutique hotel beds, Dalaman rental cars, and connecting Istanbul flights **at least 6–8 weeks early**, and avoid driving long highway stretches along the D400 on the opening Saturday morning or closing evening when domestic holidaymakers pack every single-lane pass.
