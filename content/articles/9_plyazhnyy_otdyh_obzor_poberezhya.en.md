# Beaches of the Turquoise Coast: Sand, Pebbles & Where the Water Glows Clear

First-timers landing in [Dalaman](/en/dalaman) often expect one continuous strip of powdery white sand. Instead, mountains drop straight into the sea between [Datça](/en/dacha) and [Kaş](/en/kas), shaping a coastline dominated by smooth shingle coves and rock platforms. That geology comes with a huge silver lining: with minimal loose silt clouding the surf, the water off Göcek, Kaş, and Datça stays gin-clear even in an afternoon breeze—while the region still hides four magnificent sandy sweeps long enough to exhaust any stroller.

Matching your beach expectations to the actual shore saves hours of hunting for the right towel spot.

## True Sand Beaches & Turtle Spits

If your priority is cushion-soft sand underfoot and gentle, shallow wading for kids, aim straight for these four stretches:

* **[Sarıgerme](/en/articles/sarigerme-guide) — 7 km of fine golden sand:** Just 20 minutes (16 km) from DLM Airport, Sarıgerme delivers wide, pale sand with an ultra-gradual, toddler-friendly entry. Skip expensive five-star day passes by heading to the west-end public **SARÇED Beach** (minimal entry/parking fee, clean showers, affordable beds) for the exact same shoreline.
* **[İztuzu Beach](/en/articles/iztuzu-beach) (Dalyan) — 4.5 km protected spit:** A rare geographic wonder where salty Mediterranean surf kisses one side of a golden sandbar and warm freshwater reeds of the [Dalyan](/en/dalyan) delta wash the other. Vital nesting ground for loggerhead sea turtles (*Caretta caretta*): high-rises and jet skis are banned, and from May through September/October the **beach locks strictly from 20:00 to 08:00** to protect night-nesting females and hatchlings.
* **Patara — 18 km of untamed dunes:** Turkey's longest unbroken sandy beach stretches midway between Fethiye and Kaş, accessed straight through the ancient Lycian ruins of Patara (single `muze.gov.tr` admission or MuseumPass). Afternoon winds kick up lively surf, while the windswept dunes stretch hotel-free to the horizon.
* **Cleopatra Beach on Sedir Island (Gulf of Gökova):** Famous for luminous cream-colored oolitic shell sand. As a Grade I protected archaeological sanctuary, **walking directly on the sand or laying towels across it is strictly prohibited**: visitors step down wooden boardwalks into the shallows, rinse under mandatory exit showers, and risk steep fines for pocketing sand.

## Lagoons & Pine-Framed Coves: Dalaman, Göcek & Fethiye

* **[Ölüdeniz](/en/articles/oludeniz) (Belcekız vs. the Blue Lagoon):** Know which half you're walking onto. Sweeping pebble-and-sand **Belcekız Beach** is completely free, packs punchy shorebreak waves, and serves as the landing strip for paragliders spiraling down from [Babadağ](/en/articles/babadag). Tucked to the right lies paid **Kumburnu Nature Park**—the postcard Blue Lagoon itself—where glassy water stays calm and warm even on breezy days.
* **Pine-Shaded Kıdrak:** Sitting 3 km south of Ölüdeniz, Kıdrak trades crowds and buzzing canopies for thick pine shade right behind crisp shingle and clear turquoise surf.
* **[Çalış Beach](/en/articles/calis-beach) (Fethiye):** A breezy 4-kilometer shingle-and-sand promenade boasting the region's most theatrical sunsets over offshore islands. Great for late-afternoon windsurfing and sunset dining, though depth drops relatively quickly and afternoon chop stirs up nearshore water.
* **Dalaman & Göcek Hideaways:** Escape busy boardwalks down the pine switchbacks into **[Sarsala Bay](/en/articles/sarsala-bay)** (25 minutes south of Dalaman), shade-drenched **[Kille Bay](/en/articles/kille-bay)** off the Göcek road, or the dark volcanic sands of public **İnlice Beach**.
* **[Butterfly Valley](/en/articles/butterfly-valley) & Kabak:** Dramatic gorge beaches tucked below 350-meter cliffs—catch a boat taxi from Ölüdeniz to Butterfly Valley (skip the dangerous rope climb down from Faralya above).

## Cliffs, Gorge Sands & Timber Decks: Kalkan & Kaş

East of Patara, mountains sheer straight into deep cobalt water. Downtown **[Kaş](/en/articles/kas-guide)** has zero sandy beaches—instead, swimmers lounge on sun-warmed timber platforms with metal ladders dropping straight into electric-blue water (along Çukurbağ Peninsula and central beach clubs).

For classic shoreline nearby, hit three favorites:
1. **Kaputaş (*Kaputaş Plajı*):** Highway D400's most photographed slice of bright turquoise surf sandwiched inside a golden limestone canyon—reached down **187 steep steps** from the cliff road. Arrive by **08:30–09:00** or forget roadside parking.
2. **Büyük Çakıl:** A cozy pebble crescent 1.5 km walk east of Kaş town, famous for icy freshwater springs welling right beneath swimmers even in August.
3. **Limanağzı Coves:** Sheltered waterside decks across the bay from Kaş, reached via 10-minute wooden water taxis departing the central harbor every half-hour.

## Aegean Shingle: Marmaris, İçmeler & Datça

In central **[Marmaris](/en/marmaris)**, the narrow dark-sand *Uzunyalı* strip packs tightly with hotel bars and sunbeds. Clearer swimming starts minutes west at wider, tree-backed **[İçmeler](/en/articles/icmeler)**, sheltered Blue Flag **[Turunç](/en/articles/turunc)**, or dark-sand **[Çiftlik Bay](/en/articles/ciftlik-bay)** further down the Bozburun road.

Out on the **[Datça Peninsula](/en/articles/datca-peninsula)**, beaches unwind across pristine, unhurried pebble bays facing south: 2-kilometer **Palamutbükü** with gin-clear depth, sheltered **Hayıtbükü** and **Ovabükü** wrapped in garden guesthouses, and pine-backed **Kargı Bay** right outside Datça town.

> [!TIP]
> **Insider Tip & 2026 Beach Pricing:** By Turkish law, public access to municipal beaches (*Halk Plajı*) is always free—you pay only to rent loungers and umbrellas or to enter protected state parks (such as Ölüdeniz's Kumburnu Lagoon, Kıdrak, Sarsala, and İztuzu). Two sunbeds plus a shade umbrella at public bays generally run **€5–12** in 2026, while stylish swim-deck clubs in Göcek, Ölüdeniz, or Kaş often operate a **€15–30 minimum food-and-drink spend** instead. Packing **€5–8 water shoes (*deniz ayakkabısı*)** is non-negotiable for Kaş, Çalış, Turunç, and Datça pebbles.
